# Kitchen Asset Library — Little Learning Town

## Art Style & Philosophy

Kitchen assets use **bright, clean, rounded shapes** with toy-like proportions. Appliances are colorful and friendly — no sharp metal edges, no realistic branding, no dark or intimidating designs. Colors use pastel and primary palettes. Materials (plastic, metal, ceramic) are visually distinct but softened for a child-appropriate aesthetic.

All items are designed for kitchen play sets, cooking scenes, cafeteria backgrounds, and meal-preparation sequences.

---

## Naming Convention

```
KIT_Type_Number
```

Examples: `KIT_Appliance_003`, `KIT_Utensil_012`, `KIT_Cookware_008`
