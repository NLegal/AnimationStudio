## Holiday-Specific Negatives

### What to Avoid in Holiday Elements

- **Scary Themes**: Holidays in this world are purely joyful. Avoid scary Halloween elements (realistic ghosts, gore, terror), violent themes, or frightening costumes.
- **Religious Specificity**: Keep holiday assets broadly festive and secular — avoid specific religious iconography that could limit reuse.
- **Damage & Wear**: No wilting Christmas trees, broken ornaments, melted candles, wrinkled wrapping paper, or deflated balloons.
- **Mess**: Avoid stray confetti clutter (keep tidy), frosting smears, broken eggshells, or candle wax drips.
- **Safety**: No exposed candle flames (use LED-style glow), sharp ornament hooks, or fragile decorations that could break.
- **Cultural Sensitivity**: Avoid stereotypes or caricatures. Keep decorations generic and universally child-friendly.

### Usage Notes

Use the base negative prompt for every holiday generation. Add the extended negative prompt for all holiday-specific items. For Halloween items, especially emphasize removing scary/horror elements. For Christmas and Easter, focus on maintaining fresh, undamaged decorations.
