## Food-Specific Negatives

### What to Avoid in Food

- **Spoilage**: Food in this world is always fresh, appetizing, and perfectly edible. Avoid any signs of rot, mold, or decay.
- **Over-processing**: Avoid overly realistic food textures like greasy surfaces, soggy textures, or realistic meat fibers.
- **Appeal Issues**: No unappetizing colors, slimy textures, or unidentifiable ingredients.
- **Damage**: Avoid bruised fruit, broken cookies, melted ice cream (unless intentional), or crushed items.
- **Cross-contamination**: Avoid mixing incompatible foods or unrealistic food combinations.
- **Unsafe Elements**: No exposed bones, sharp food fragments, or choking hazards.

### Usage Notes

Use the base negative prompt for every food generation. Add the extended negative prompt when generating perishable items like fruit, dairy, or baked goods. For processed foods like packaged snacks, you may reduce the spoilage-related negatives.
