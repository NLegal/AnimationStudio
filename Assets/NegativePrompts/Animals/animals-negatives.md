## Animal-Specific Negatives

### What to Avoid in Animals

- **Frightening Traits**: Animals must be cute, friendly, and non-threatening. Avoid aggressive poses, bared teeth, snarling, or hissing.
- **Realistic Anatomy**: Avoid hyper-realistic fur, detailed muscle definition, visible bone structure, or realistic animal proportions.
- **Uncanny Elements**: No glassy eyes, overly complex fur patterns, or unsettlingly realistic animal faces.
- **Mess**: Avoid drooling, muddy fur, matted hair, or dirty paws.
- **Injuries**: No limping, wounds, bandages, missing limbs, or sickly appearances.
- **Scale Distortion**: Maintain consistent scale — animals should be character-appropriate size, not giant or tiny versions.
- **Expression**: Avoid sad, angry, or scared expressions. Animals should be happy and approachable.

### Usage Notes

Use the base negative prompt for every animal generation. Add the extended negative prompt for all animal types to ensure cute, friendly results. For wild animals, emphasize "frightening" and "aggressive" negatives more heavily. For farm animals and pets, focus on "dirty" and "messy" negatives.
