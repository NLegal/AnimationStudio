## Furniture-Specific Negatives

### What to Avoid in Furniture

- **Damage & Wear**: Furniture in this world is always sturdy, clean, and well-maintained. Avoid scratches, dents, stains, or wobbling legs.
- **Age Issues**: No peeling paint, water rings, sun-faded upholstery, or sagging cushions.
- **Safety Hazards**: Avoid sharp corners (furniture must have rounded edges), exposed nails/screws, unstable construction.
- **Style Consistency**: Avoid overly realistic wood grain patterns, complex Victorian ornamentation, or stark modern/minimalist designs.
- **Scale Problems**: Avoid adult-sized furniture mixed with child-scale items — maintain consistent world scale.
- **Upholstery Issues**: No ripped fabric, torn leather, sprung coils, or flattened cushions.

### Usage Notes

Use the base negative prompt for every furniture generation. Add the extended negative prompt when generating upholstered furniture or wooden pieces. For plastic children's furniture, emphasize "cheap plastic" and "flimsy" in the negatives.
