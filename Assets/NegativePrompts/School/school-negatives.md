## School-Specific Negatives

### What to Avoid in School Supplies

- **Damage**: School supplies must look new and functional. Avoid broken pencils, torn pages, cracked bindings, or empty ink.
- **Wear & Tear**: No chewed pencil ends, wrinkled papers, bent covers, or smudged erasers.
- **Cleanliness**: Avoid dirty erasers, stained notebooks, sticky tape residue, or fingerprinted surfaces.
- **Safety**: No exposed metal spirals on notebooks, sharp compass points, or broken ruler edges.
- **Functionality**: Avoid missing parts like pencil erasers, broken zippers on backpacks, or torn pocket linings.
- **Realism**: Avoid overly realistic paper textures or photocopied/printed worksheet looks.

### Usage Notes

Use the base negative prompt for every school supply generation. Add the extended negative prompt when generating paper-based items, backpacks, or writing tools. Remove "text" from negatives if the item intentionally has text (e.g., educational flashcards).
