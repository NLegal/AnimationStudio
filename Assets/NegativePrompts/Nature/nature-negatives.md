## Nature-Specific Negatives

### What to Avoid in Nature Elements

- **Decay**: Nature in this world is vibrant, healthy, and friendly. Avoid dead leaves, wilted flowers, rotting wood, or diseased plants.
- **Harshness**: Avoid thorny plants, jagged rocks, spiky branches, or anything that could hurt a child.
- **Intimidation**: No scary-looking trees, dark forests, threatening animals, or ominous weather.
- **Realism**: Avoid hyper-realistic textures like detailed insect anatomy, muddy surfaces, or complex organic imperfections.
- **Seasonal Inappropriateness**: Maintain the cheerful style — avoid dead winter trees (unless intentional), barren landscapes, or storm damage.
- **Pests**: Avoid visible insects that could be considered frightening (spiders, wasps), but friendly bugs (ladybugs, butterflies) are welcome.

### Usage Notes

Use the base negative prompt for every nature generation. Add the extended negative prompt when generating plants, trees, or outdoor scenes. For friendly insects and small creatures, remove "insect" and "bug" related negatives and use category-appropriate ones instead.
