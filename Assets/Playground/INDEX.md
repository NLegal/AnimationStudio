# Playground Asset Library

Playground equipment is defined by rounded shapes, sturdy construction, bright primary colors, and a safety-first design philosophy. Every piece is built for durability and imaginative play, with soft edges, stable bases, and cheerful aesthetics that invite children to climb, swing, and explore.
