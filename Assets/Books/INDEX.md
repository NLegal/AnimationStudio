# Book Asset Library — Little Learning Town

## Design Philosophy

Books in Little Learning Town are designed to look **inviting, durable, and distinctly child-friendly** — the kind of books a preschooler would pull off the shelf again and again. Every book cover is crafted with **bold typography**, **bright illustrations**, and **rounded physical edges** (board books) or **sturdy laminated covers** (paperbacks). The visual style matches the broader Little Learning Town aesthetic: Pixar-quality illustrations, consistent character designs, and a warm, welcoming color palette.

## Cover Design Standards

- **Bold Typography** — Large, rounded sans-serif titles with high contrast against backgrounds. Title is always readable at arm's length.
- **Character-Driven** — Little Learning Town characters appear on covers to build brand recognition.
- **Bright Color Schemes** — Covers use 2-4 dominant colors with strong visual hierarchy.
- **Rounded Corners** — Board books have physically rounded corners; paperbacks use rounded cover design elements.
- **Age-Appropriate Art** — Simple, clear illustrations with emotional warmth and visual appeal.
- **Size Variation** — Board books (18 × 18 cm), Picture books (22 × 28 cm), Activity books (25 × 20 cm).

## Quality Standards

| Standard | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Durability | Laminated or board-book construction for classroom use |
| Safety | Rounded corners, non-toxic inks, no small detachable parts |
| Readability | Font size minimum 18 pt, high contrast text |
| Illustration Quality | Full-color, Pixar-quality artwork consistent with universe |
| Brand Consistency | Little Learning Town logo on back cover or spine |
| Age Appropriateness | Content and vocabulary matched to listed reading level |
| Page Count | Appropriate for age group (board: 10-20 pages, picture: 24-36 pages) |

## Reading Level Key

| Level | Age | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Baby | 0-2 | Board books, simple images, high contrast |
| Toddler | 2-3 | Simple sentences, repetitive text, familiar objects |
| Preschool | 3-5 | Short stories, basic concepts, rhyme |
| Early Reader | 5-6 | Simple words, picture cues, short sentences |
| Read Aloud | 3-7 | Longer stories meant to be read by an adult |
