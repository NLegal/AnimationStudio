# Food Asset Library — Little Learning Town

## Art Style & Philosophy

All food assets follow a **child-friendly, bright, colorful** design language inspired by Pixar and Cocomelon. No realistic textures, no dark or burnt food, no sharp edges. Colors are saturated but warm. Shapes are clean, rounded, and immediately readable. Food should look **appetizing, safe, and fun** — never messy, spoiled, or complex.

Every asset is designed for reuse across meal scenes, grocery stores, cooking playsets, and picnic environments.

---

## Naming Convention

```
FOOD_Type_Number
```

Examples: `FOOD_Fruit_001`, `FOOD_Meal_012`, `FOOD_Dessert_005`
