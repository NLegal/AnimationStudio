# Animal Library

**Style Guide:** All animals are cute, rounded, friendly, and highly expressive. Inspired by Cocomelon and Pixar — oversized eyes, soft shapes, warm colors, and gentle proportions. No aggressive postures, sharp teeth, or scary features. Every animal should look like it wants to be friends and is safe for the youngest viewers.

**Scale Reference:** Small (under 30cm), Medium (30–100cm), Large (over 100cm).
