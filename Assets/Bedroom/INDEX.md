# Bedroom Asset Library — Little Learning Town

## Art Style & Philosophy

Bedroom assets are **cozy, warm, and whimsical**. Soft pastels, rounded furniture edges, and friendly shapes create a space children would love. Nothing should feel sharp, heavy, or adult. Textures are soft (fabric) or smoothly painted (wood/plastic). The bedroom should feel like a **safe imaginative haven** for play, rest, and learning.

Designed for bedroom scenes, sleepover sequences, morning routines, and cozy reading nooks.

---

## Naming Convention

```
BED_Type_Number
```

Examples: `BED_Furniture_008`, `BED_Decor_012`, `BED_Storage_005`
