# Living Room Asset Library — Little Learning Town

## Art Style & Philosophy

Living room assets are **warm, inviting, and family-friendly**. Furniture has plush rounded forms in soft earthy and pastel colors. Decor items add personality without clutter. Electronics are simplified with friendly interfaces and no sharp corners. The living room should feel like the **heart of the home** — a space for family time, play, and relaxation.

Designed for living room scenes, family gatherings, TV time, reading corners, and indoor play sequences.

---

## Naming Convention

```
LIV_Type_Number
```

Examples: `LIV_Furniture_007`, `LIV_Decor_011`, `LIV_Electronic_004`
