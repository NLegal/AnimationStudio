# Holiday Asset Library

**Style Guide:** Festive, bright, and child-friendly. No scary elements, no gore, no horror motifs. Pumpkins are cheerful, ghosts are friendly, bats are cute, and skeletons are smiling. Colors are saturated and joyful. Every decoration should feel like a party waiting to happen.
