## Overview

Happy Hills Park is the most active zone in Little Learning Town, a sprawling recreational area built on gentle slopes with soft, rubberized grass surfaces. The playground equipment is oversized, rounded, and impossibly colorful — slides that look like twisting rainbows, swings shaped like rocket ships, and a climbing wall covered in friendly monster faces. A splash pad with ground-level fountains keeps things cool in summer. The park is dotted with picnic tables beneath giant mushroom-shaped umbrellas, and a dedicated bike trail winds through the area.

**Color Palette:** Lime green (#32CD32), tangerine (#FFA500), turquoise (#40E0D0), magenta, sunny yellow

**Design Notes:** All equipment is child-safe with rounded edges, soft landings, and no sharp corners. Ground surfaces are rubberized mulch or soft synthetic grass. Everything is scaled for ages 2-8.

## Main Playground Structure
**Identifier:** ENV_Playground_001

**Description:** The centerpiece of Happy Hills Park — a massive, multi-level play structure that combines slides, climbing elements, bridges, and interactive panels into one cohesive unit. The structure is shaped like a friendly castle with rounded towers and rainbow-colored segments.

**Structure Overview:**
- **Base Level (Ground):** A series of platforms at ground level with steering wheels, telescopes, and interactive panels. Accessible for toddlers.
- **Mid Level (3ft high):** Connected by ramps and low bridges. Features a bubble window and a spiral slide.
- **Upper Level (5ft high):** The tallest point, accessible by a gentle ramp and a climbing net. Features a telescope and a curved tube slide.

**Slides:**
- A twisting rainbow tube slide (yellow, orange, pink, purple segments) — 12ft long, fully enclosed with translucent sections.
- A wide wave slide (blue with white wave patterns) — 8ft long, open.
- A short toddler slide (green, 4ft long) with gentle slope.
- A double slide (side-by-side red and blue) for racing.

**Other Elements:**
- A suspension bridge with rope handrails.
- A tunnel crawl-through (translucent, shows shadows).
- Interactive panels: a gear wall, a bead maze, a periscope.
- A pretend storefront with a window and counter.

**Safety:** Rubberized safety surface in deep purple with embedded rubber tiles. The entire structure is surrounded by a 6ft fall zone of soft mulch.

## Splash Pad
**Identifier:** ENV_Playground_008

**Description:** A zero-depth water play area with ground-level fountains and spray features. The surface is textured non-slip paving in light blue with wave patterns.

**Features:**
- Ground spray jets that cycle through different patterns (tulip, fan, geyser, mist).
- A tipping bucket (a large blue bucket that fills and dumps gently).
- Animal-shaped sprayers: a whale that spouts, a frog that squirts, a flower that mists.
- Water temperature is regulated (warm, never cold).
- A drain cover that is flush with the ground (no tripping hazard).
- Shade structures around the perimeter.
- Benches for parents at the edge (splash-free zone).
- A button to activate the splash pad (child-height).

**Seasonal:** Open from May through September. Converts to a dry play area in cooler months.

**Characters:** All children love the splash pad. Daisy Duck is queen here. Dog (Pup) chases the water jets.

## Mini Golf
**Identifier:** ENV_Playground_011

**Description:** A 9-hole miniature golf course with whimsical obstacles designed for young children. The course winds through a garden-like setting.

**Hole Themes:**
1. **Bunny Burrow** — hit through a carrot-shaped tunnel.
2. **Whale's Splash** — putt around a small water feature (shallow).
3. **Castle Gate** — through a castle archway.
4. **Windmill** — around a spinning windmill blade.
5. **Dragon's Cave** — through a friendly dragon's mouth.
6. **Flower Garden** — through a series of flower pots.
7. **Pirate Ship** — around a small wooden ship.
8. **Rainbow Bridge** — over a small hill.
9. **Star Finish** — into a giant star-shaped hole.

**Features:**
- Child-sized putters (various colors) and light balls.
- A scorecard with pictures instead of numbers.
- A "Hole in One" bell to ring.
- Astroturf surface in bright green.
- Low borders (no frustration for little ones).

**Characters:** Ben Bear takes it seriously. Monkey uses creative (silly) techniques. Lily Bunny enjoys the flower hole.

## Skate Park
**Identifier:** ENV_Playground_012

**Description:** A small skate park with smooth concrete surfaces and gentle transitions. Designed for scooters, skateboards, and bikes.

**Features:**
- A half-pipe ramp (3ft high, gentle curve).
- A quarter pipe (2ft high).
- A rail (low, 6 inches off ground) for grinding practice.
- A flat box (2ft x 4ft) for trick practice.
- A small bowl (2ft deep, 8ft diameter) — for advanced users.
- Helmet and pad rental kiosk (adjacent).
- Benches for spectators.

**Safety:** Full protective gear required. A posted "Safety Rules" board with illustrated instructions. A supervisor station during peak hours.

**Characters:** Horse (Dash) rides a scooter here. Dog (Pup) tries skateboarding. Monkey practices tricks.

## Bike Trail
**Identifier:** ENV_Playground_013

**Description:** A winding paved trail (1.5 miles long) that loops through Happy Hills Park and connects to the Meadow Path. Suitable for tricycles, bicycles with training wheels, scooters, and walking.

**Features:**
- Smooth paved surface in warm gray.
- Lane markings (bike icons, arrows).
- Gentle hills (no steep inclines).
- Traffic signs (child-friendly: "Stop," "Yield," "Slow").
- Rest stops with benches and water fountains every quarter mile.
- Mile markers with fun facts ("You've traveled 200 bunny hops!").
- A tunnel under the main path (lit with color-changing lights).
- A small bridge over a decorative stream.
- Wildflower borders on both sides.

**Characters:** Charlie Fox rides his bike here. Lily Bunny uses her tricycle. Daisy Duck scooters. Family bike rides on weekends.

## Camping Area
**Identifier:** ENV_Playground_014

**Description:** A designated camping zone in a secluded corner of Happy Hills Park, surrounded by pine trees and a small meadow.

**Features:**
- Three raised wooden tent platforms (12ft x 12ft).

**Activities:** Family camping nights, school campouts, stargazing events.

**Characters:** Charlie Fox loves camping here. Grandpa Bunny takes the children for overnight adventures. Teacher Owl hosts class campouts.

## Restrooms and Drinking Fountain
**Identifier:** ENV_Playground_015

**Description:** A central pavilion housing restrooms and water facilities. The building is a rounded structure with a green roof and stone-colored walls.

**Restrooms:**
- Family restroom (large, single-occupancy, with changing table).
- Boys' restroom (two stalls, two low urinals, two sinks).
- Girls' restroom (three stalls, three sinks).
- Child-height sinks and toilets in all restrooms.
- Step stools available.
- Automatic soap dispensers and faucets.
- Bright, clean, well-lit with cheerful tile work.
- A fish tank in the entrance area.

**Drinking Fountain:**
- Two-tier fountain (adult height and child height).
- A bottle-filling station with a count of "Plastic bottles saved."
- A pet fountain at ground level (small bowl with running water).
- Water is filtered and cool.

**Additional Features:**
- A lost child meeting point sign at the pavilion entrance.
- A first aid station (basic supplies).
- A bulletin board with park events and safety information.
- A "Park Rules" sign with friendly illustrations.
