# Background Asset Library — Little Learning Town

## Introduction

The Background Asset Library provides reusable environmental backdrops that form the visual foundation of every scene in Little Learning Town. Rather than generating skies, landscapes, and ground textures from scratch for each episode, artists and AI workflows compose scenes by layering these prefabricated background elements with character and prop assets.

Backgrounds are designed to be:
- **Clean and bright** — no clutter, dirt, or visual noise
- **Consistent in style** — all share the same painting technique, color philosophy, and level of detail
- **No text on surfaces** — skies, landscapes, and textures never include written words, logos, or watermarks
- **Modular** — skies can swap independently of landscapes, textures can layer over landscapes
- **Seamless** — landscape and texture edges tile or blend naturally into adjacent elements

## Quality Standards

- Resolution: minimum 1920×1080 (4K preferred for future-proofing)
- No text, watermarks, logos, or branding of any kind
- No people, characters, or interactive props in the background (these are separate layers)
- Color palette matches the approved world palette (bright, warm, pastel-friendly)
- Soft gradients — no harsh color transitions
- Appropriate level of detail for the intended camera distance
- All backgrounds are safe for children (no frightening imagery, even in storm/night variants)

## Layering System

Scenes are built in three background layers:

1. **Skies** — The topmost layer, fills the upper portion of frame. Sets mood, time of day, and weather.
2. **Landscapes** — The middle layer, defines the horizon and distant geography. Creates depth and place.
3. **Textures** — The ground layer, applied below characters' feet. Defines the surface they stand on.

Skies and landscapes can be mixed freely. Textures can be swapped independently of the landscape for indoor/outdoor flexibility.

# Appendix: Background Composition Guide

When building a scene, combine layers in this order:

1. **Sky** — Select based on time of day and weather
2. **Landscape** — Select based on location
3. **Textures** — Select based on ground surface type
4. **Props** — Add from the Prop Library as needed
5. **Characters** — Place on the texture layer

**Example combinations:**

| Scene | Sky | Landscape | Texture |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sunny park play | BG_Sky_002 | BG_Landscape_001 | BG_Texture_001 |
| Beach day | BG_Sky_002 | BG_Landscape_004 | BG_Texture_002 |
| Bedtime story | BG_Sky_005 | BG_Landscape_001 | BG_Texture_007 |
| Snowy winter | BG_Sky_007 | BG_Landscape_007 | BG_Texture_008 |
| Farm visit | BG_Sky_002 | BG_Landscape_005 | BG_Texture_001 |
| Classroom indoor | — | — | BG_Texture_005 |

For indoor scenes, landscapes and skies are typically replaced by wall/ceiling backgrounds. Use floor textures as the ground layer and wall backgrounds as needed.
