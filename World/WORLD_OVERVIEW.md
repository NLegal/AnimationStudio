## World Name

### Little Learning Town

A place where every street leads to discovery, every building smiles, and every day brings a new adventure. Little Learning Town is the permanent, reusable setting for all episodes produced by the AI Nursery Rhyme Studio. It is never referred to as simply "the town" — it is always **Little Learning Town**.

---

## Design Philosophy

Little Learning Town is built on nine core pillars that guide every creative decision:

| Pillar | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Safe** | Every space is child-proofed, gentle, and hazard-free. No sharp edges, no steep drops, no scary corners. |
| **Bright** | High-key lighting, sunny skies, and luminous colors define every scene. Shadows are soft and never menacing. |
| **Warm** | The palette leans toward warm yellows, peaches, soft oranges, and gentle pinks. The town feels like a hug. |
| **Colorful** | Every zone uses a distinct, vibrant color identity. No muted grays or dull browns dominate any frame. |
| **Educational** | Environments are designed to teach — numbers on mailboxes, alphabet on school walls, shapes in playground equipment. |
| **Curious** | Hidden details encourage exploration. A butterfly in the garden, a secret path in the woods, a shiny shell on the beach. |
| **Friendly** | Every building has a welcoming entrance. Every NPC (animal, shopkeeper) is approachable. Nothing is intimidating. |
| **Clean** | Streets are spotless, gardens are manicured, buildings are well-maintained. The town takes pride in itself. |
| **Optimistic** | The sun is always shining somewhere in Little Learning Town. Even rainy days end with rainbows. |

---

## World Rules

The following restrictions are absolute. No environment, prop, or background may violate these rules:

| Rule | Rationale |
|------|-----------|
| No realistic cities | The world is a storybook, not a documentary. Architecture is stylized, rounded, and fantastical. |
| No abandoned buildings | Every structure is actively used, loved, and cared for. No boarded windows or "for sale" signs. |
| No graffiti | Walls are clean or decorated with intentional murals. No vandalism exists in this world. |
| No broken roads | Pavement is smooth, sidewalks are even, crosswalks are clearly painted. |
| No dangerous environments | No cliffs without railings, no traffic without crosswalks, no wild animals acting aggressively, no deep water without supervision. |
| No frightening weather | Storms are gentle. Thunder is soft. Lightning is distant. Snow is fluffy, not blizzard. Rain comes with umbrellas and puddles to splash in. |
| No weapons or violence | The world is conflict-free. Problems are solved through cooperation, not force. |
| No decay or pollution | The air is clean, the water is clear, the grass is green. Industrial elements are absent or stylized as toys. |

### 1. Sunny Meadow (Residential Zone)
**Identifier:** ENV_Residential_###

**Color Palette:** Soft yellows, warm greens, buttercream, peach, lavender

The heart of Little Learning Town where all families live. Sunny Meadow is a gently rolling neighborhood of rounded cottages with colorful front doors, picket fences, and perfectly manicured lawns. Every house has a garden bursting with flowers, a driveway with a family car, and a mailbox painted with the family's name. Winding sidewalks connect the homes, shaded by large friendly trees with low-hanging branches perfect for climbing. Small neighborhood parks dot the area with benches, birdbaths, and community vegetable gardens. The streets are quiet and safe, with painted crosswalks and oversized street signs that help children learn colors and shapes.

## Design Standards

### Architecture Style

| Element | Standard |
|---------|----------|
| Building shape | Rounded corners, curved roofs, no sharp angles |
| Windows | Arch-topped or circular, oversized, with warm-lit interiors at night |
| Doors | Rounded arch doors, colorful, with oversized handles |
| Steps | Wide, shallow risers (child-scale) |
| Rooflines | Curved tiles, gently sloping, often with decorative finials |
| Fences | Picket-style with rounded tops, painted white or pastel |
| Materials | Storybook textures — smooth stucco, painted wood, never brick or concrete |

### Landscaping Standards

- Grass: Lush, vibrant, uniform — always a healthy green with subtle variation
- Trees: Rounded canopies, thick trunks, friendly shapes — no bare or thorny trees
- Flowers: Oversized blooms, solid colors, clustered in groups of 3–5 varieties
- Pathways: Smooth paved or compacted earth with defined edges
- Water features: Crystal clear, shallow where children access, surrounded by rounded stones

### Visual Quality Standards

Every environment must meet the following production benchmarks:

- **Pixar-quality:** Subsurface scattering on skin, translucency on leaves, ambient occlusion in corners, physically based rendering materials
- **Cocomelon-inspired:** Bright, saturated colors, simple shapes, clean compositions, child-centric scale, musical motifs in decorative elements
- **Cinematic lighting:** Three-point lighting setup with warm key light, soft fill, and rim light for depth
- **Highly detailed:** Enough texture and small details (leaves, flowers, sign text, window reflections) to feel rich without being noisy
- **Child-safe:** No choking hazards visible, no dangerous gaps, no sharp edges

## Visual Identity Guide

### Color Philosophy

Little Learning Town uses a **warm-color-dominant** palette. Warm hues (yellows, oranges, pinks, reds) occupy 60% of any frame. Cool colors (blues, greens, purples) serve as balancing accents at 30%. Neutrals (cream, white, beige) make up the remaining 10%. This ratio ensures every scene feels bright, inviting, and energetic.

### Shapes & Silhouettes

- **Primary shape:** Circles and curves — the town's visual signature
- **Secondary shape:** Soft squares with rounded corners — for buildings and furniture
- **Tertiary shape:** Gentle triangles — used sparingly in rooflines and tree shapes
- **No sharp or jagged shapes** appear anywhere in the environment

### Typography

- All signage uses rounded, friendly sans-serif fonts
- Text is large, legible, and minimal
- Letters often appear in multiple colors on the same sign
- Decorative elements (stars, hearts, flowers) surround signage

### Lighting Standards

| Time of Day | Key Light Temp | Mood |
|-------------|---------------|------|
| Morning | 4000K (neutral warm) | Fresh, energetic |
| Noon | 5500K (daylight) | Bright, clear |
| Golden Hour | 3200K (warm) | Cozy, magical |
| Evening | 2700K (very warm) | Calm, sleepy |
| Night | 2500K with moonlight blue | Peaceful, dreamy |

### Scale Reference

All environments are built at **child scale** — approximately 30% smaller than real-world proportions. Doors are 4 feet tall. Windows start at 2 feet from the ground. Counters reach a child's waist. This perspective makes the world feel safe and empowering for the target audience.
