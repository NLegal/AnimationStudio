### Snow

### Wind
damaging wind, flying debris, fallen trees, dangerous wind, structural damage, roof torn off, broken windows from wind, flying objects, projectile, toppling signs, downed power lines, swinging dangerously, umbrella destroyed, hat blown away (unless comedic), struggling to walk, knocked over, wind chill, dangerous gusts, tornado, cyclone, gale force, hurricane force, dust cloud, sand blasting, windburn, debris hitting, tree branch breaking, falling branch, loose shingles flying, garbage cans tumbling, bike knocked over, fence blown down

### Fog
thick fog (unless intentional), low visibility, dangerous fog, fog so thick cannot see, contaminated fog, smog, toxic fog, chemical fog, radioactive fog, fog mixed with smoke, eerie fog, supernatural fog, fog that hides danger, pea soup fog, freezing fog, fog with poor visibility for safety, disorienting fog, complete whiteout, fog at night creating danger

### Thunderstorms
lightning strike, thunder too loud, dangerous lightning, close lightning, fire from lightning, tree struck by lightning, power line down, electrocution risk, sudden flash, booming thunder (scary level), rolling thunder (if too menacing), storm too intense, tornado spawn, hail damage, roof damage, flooding, creek rising, road washout, fear of storm, hiding, crying from storm

### Heat / Sunny
extreme heat, heat wave, sunburn, dehydration, heat stroke, scorching, blazing, burning, cracked earth, drought, wilted plants, dying grass, animals suffering, heat exhaustion, too hot to play, sweating heavily, red skin, sun poisoning, UV danger, no shade, hot surfaces burning skin, heat shimmer distorting view, mirage, asphalt melting, heat cracking roads

### Rainbow
too faint, washed out, incomplete arc, double rainbow (unless needed), stormy background with rainbow, rainbow at night, unrealistic placement, flat rainbow, no color transition, broken spectrum

## Usage

Combine with BASE_NEGATIVE when generating weather-specific images:

```
Negative: [BASE_NEGATIVE], extreme weather, hurricane, tornado, blizzard, flood, hail, lightning strike
```
