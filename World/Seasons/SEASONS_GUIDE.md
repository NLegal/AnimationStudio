# Seasons Guide

## Design Philosophy

Every season in Little Learning Town is **bright, colorful, and inviting**. No dead landscapes, depressing gray winters, or messy weather. Each season brings new colors, activities, and visual opportunities. Seasonal changes help children learn about the passage of time.

---

## Spring

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Description** | The world awakens. Trees blossom with pink and white flowers. Grass turns bright green. Baby animals appear. Flowers bloom everywhere. Gentle rain showers. Everything feels new and fresh. |
| **Color Palette** | Soft pink (blossoms), bright green (new leaves), pastel yellow (daffodils), light blue (sky), purple (lilacs), white (daisies), soft brown |
| **Typical Locations Changed** | School garden blooming, park trees in blossom, farm with baby animals, flower fields in color, nature trail with new growth |
| **Prompt Template** | `spring season, cherry blossoms blooming, bright green grass, fresh spring flowers, baby animals playing, soft pastel colors, spring sunshine, gentle warm weather, new leaves on trees, colorful spring garden, fresh green nature, springtime magic` |
