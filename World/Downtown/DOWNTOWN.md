## Overview

Main Street is the bustling center of Little Learning Town where families shop, eat, and gather. It is a single charming avenue lined with two-story storefronts, all featuring striped awnings, window boxes overflowing with flowers, and cobblestone-style sidewalks. The street is closed to through traffic — only bicycles, scooters, and the occasional delivery truck are allowed. A central town square features a gazebo, a fountain with shimmering coins, and oversized benches shaped like animals. Every shop has a distinct personality visible from the street: the bakery wafts sweet scents, the flower shop spills blooms onto the sidewalk, and the music store fills the air with gentle melodies.

**Color Palette:** Coral (#FF7F6E), sky blue (#87CEEB), mint green (#98FB98), lemon yellow, cream

**Design Notes:** Buildings are two stories with false fronts (storybook style). Awnings are striped. Windows have flower boxes. Signs use friendly rounded fonts with icons.

## Town Square

### Town Hall
**Identifier:** ENV_Downtown_001

**Architecture:** The centerpiece of Main Street. A three-story building with warm coral stucco walls and a tall clock tower with a rounded sky-blue roof. The entrance is a grand double-door arch in dark wood with brass handles shaped like stars. Large arched windows on the ground floor. A balcony on the second floor with a wrought-iron railing decorated with heart-shaped scrollwork.

**Clock Tower:** The tower has a large round clock face with golden numerals and decorative hands. The clock chimes hourly with a gentle melody (not loud). A weather vane shaped like a flying book sits atop the spire.

**Features:**
- A wide front porch with four rounded columns.
- A bulletin board posting community events.
- Flower beds along the foundation with red and white tulips.
- Two lantern-style lights flanking the entrance.
- A sign reading "Little Learning Town Hall" with the town motto "Learn, Play, Grow."

**Interior:**
- **Lobby:** A grand foyer with a high ceiling, a large circular chandelier shaped like a sunburst, and a curved welcome desk. The floor is polished cream marble with a compass rose pattern in the center. Benches line the walls.
- **Community Meeting Room:** A large room with a high ceiling, rounded windows, and a stage at one end. Used for town meetings, birthday parties, and community events. Colorful bunting hangs from the ceiling.
- **Records Office:** Where important town documents are kept. Friendly staff help residents.
- **Mayor's Office:** A sunny corner office with two windows overlooking the square. A round wooden desk, bookshelves, and a small meeting table.

**Characters:** Policer Officer Beaver visits daily. Firefighter Dalmatian checks in weekly. Teacher Owl hosts community events here.

### Town Square & Gazebo
**Identifier:** ENV_Downtown_014

**Description:** The central gathering space of Main Street. A circular paved area with cream-colored stones arranged in a sunburst pattern. In the center stands a white gazebo with a rounded sky-blue roof, delicate white scrollwork, and steps on all four sides.

**Features:**
- String lights crisscross above the gazebo.
- Flower baskets hanging from the gazebo eaves.
- A small stage inside for performances.
- Benches around the perimeter.

### Wishing Fountain
**Identifier:** ENV_Downtown_016

**Description:** A three-tiered fountain in the shape of a giant flower, located at the edge of the Town Square. Water cascades gently from the top tier into a wide basin. The basin is shallow (child-safe) and lined with colorful mosaic tiles depicting fish, stars, and flowers.

**Features:**
- Shimmering coins at the bottom (wishes).
- A sign: "Make a Wish!" with a star icon.
- Benches surround the fountain.
- Children are supervised when near the water.
- Rainbow-colored water jets at night (gentle lighting).

### Animal-Shaped Benches

**Variants:**

## Shops & Services

### Library
**Identifier:** ENV_Downtown_002

**Exterior Features:**
- A return slot shaped like a bookshelf.

- A bike rack shaped like open books.

**Interior:**

**Characters:** Librarian Hedgehog (Miss Quills) works here. Teacher Owl visits for story time. Lily Bunny and Ben Bear are regulars.
