# SCHOOL — Little Learning Academy

**Production:** AI Nursery Rhyme Studio

**Phase:** 2 — World Building & Environment Bible
**Zone:** Little Learning Academy (Education Zone)

---

## Overview

Little Learning Academy is the educational heart of Little Learning Town. It is a cheerful school complex with a rainbow-colored facade, oversized clocks, and windows shaped like books. The main building features a curved entrance with the school's motto "Learn, Play, Grow" written in colorful block letters. The campus includes multiple specialized buildings connected by covered walkways, all surrounded by flowering shrubs and shady trees. A large outdoor classroom with log benches and a chalkboard tree trunk makes learning beneath the sun a daily joy.

**Color Palette:** Primary red (#FF4444), bright yellow (#FFD700), cobalt blue (#0047AB), white, apple green

**Design Notes:** Everything is child-scale. Windows start at 2ft from ground. Doors are 4ft tall. Furniture is appropriately sized for preschool and kindergarten children. Hallways are wide and brightly lit.

## School Exterior
**Identifier:** ENV_School_001

**Architecture:** A single-story main building (with a second-floor mezzanine in the central hall) stretching in a gentle U-shape around a central courtyard. The facade is a patchwork of warm colors — red, yellow, blue, and green sections divided by white trim. The roof is a gentle slope with blue curved tiles.

**Main Entrance:** A grand rounded archway framed by two columns shaped like giant pencils (yellow with pink erasers). Above the entrance, the school motto "LEARN • PLAY • GROW" appears in large colorful block letters. A large round clock with multi-colored numbers sits above the motto.

**Features:**
- Windows shaped like open books with rounded corners.
- Flower beds along the entire front foundation with red and yellow tulips.
- A flagpole with the school flag (rainbow background, open book emblem).
- A wide paved drop-off zone with "Kiss and Go" sign.
- A bicycle parking area with racks shaped like crayons.
- A large welcome sign: "Little Learning Academy — Everyone is Welcome Here!"

**Pathways:** Paved walkways connect the main building to the Preschool Wing and the Outdoor Classroom. Walkways are covered by colorful pergolas with climbing vines.

## Interior

### Hallway
**Identifier:** ENV_School_002

**Description:** The main hallway runs along the inside of the U-shaped building, with classrooms on one side and windows facing the courtyard on the other. The hallway is wide (8ft), brightly lit with warm overhead lights, and features a glossy wooden floor in a warm honey color.

**Decor:**
- Student artwork displayed on every wall at child-height.
- The ceiling has cloud-shaped light fixtures.
- A "Lost Tooth" chart near the Nurse's Office.
- A "Birthday Board" with each student's name and birth date.
- Hooks and cubbies along one wall for backpacks and coats (each cubby labeled with a student's name and animal icon).
- A colorful number line runs along the wall at child-eye-level.
- An alphabet border near the ceiling with illustrated letters (A for Apple, B for Bear, etc.).

**Notable:** The hallway has a designated "Quiet Zone" near the Library and a "Gallery Walk" section near the Art Room where the best artwork is displayed monthly.

### Classroom — Preschool (Ages 3-4)
**Identifier:** ENV_School_003

**Teacher:** Teacher Owl (Mr. Owl)

**Description:** A warm, inviting room with soft apple-green walls and a cream-colored ceiling. The room is divided into learning centers around a central carpet area.

**Learning Centers:**
- **Circle Time Corner:** A large round rainbow rug where students gather for morning meetings. A wooden easel with a whiteboard and alphabet chart. Teacher Owl's rocking chair.
- **Reading Nook:** A cozy corner with a small bookshelf, floor cushions shaped like animals, and a fabric canopy overhead.
- **Block Center:** Shelves of wooden blocks in various shapes and colors. A low table for building.
- **Art Easel:** Two child-height easels with paper, paint cups, and brushes.
- **Dramatic Play Area:** A small kitchen set, dress-up clothes rack, and a mirror.
- **Discovery Table:** A low table with rotating science/nature items (seashells, leaves, magnets).
- **Listening Center:** Headphones and a player with audiobooks and music.

**Furniture:** Small wooden tables and chairs in primary colors. Low open shelving. A cubby wall for storage.

**Windows:** Two large arched windows overlooking the school garden. Colorful sheer curtains.

**Class Size:** 10-12 students (Lily Bunny, Ben Bear, Daisy Duck, Charlie Fox, Monkey, Cat, Pig, Cow, Horse, Dog, Chicken, Sheep, Elephant).

### Classroom — Kindergarten (Ages 5-6)
**Identifier:** ENV_School_004

**Teacher:** Teacher Owl (Mr. Owl) with occasional guest teachers

**Description:** Slightly more structured than the preschool room, with desks instead of tables. Walls are a warm sky-blue. The room has a more academic feel while remaining fun and colorful.

**Features:**
- Individual child-sized desks with rounded corners, arranged in clusters of four.
- A large smart board / interactive whiteboard at the front.
- A classroom library with leveled readers.
- A math corner with counting tools, abacuses, and number lines.
- A writing center with paper, pencils, and word walls.
- A science observation table with magnifying glasses and plants.
- A "Star Student" board featuring a different child each week.

**Decor:** Alphabet and number charts. A world map with animals marking different continents. A class rules poster with illustrated guidelines (friendly faces, gentle hands, listening ears).

### Classroom — Pre-K (Ages 4-5)
**Identifier:** ENV_School_005

**Teacher:** Teacher Owl (Mr. Owl)

**Description:** A bright yellow-walled classroom designed for the transitional year between preschool and kindergarten. Combines play-based learning with early academic skills.

**Features:**
- Half tables, half desks — transitioning from preschool setup.
- A sensory bin table (rotating contents: rice, sand, water beads).
- A writing readiness center with tracing activities.
- A math manipulative shelf with counters, pattern blocks, and puzzles.
- A quiet corner with weighted blankets and calming bottles.
- A "Question of the Day" board.

## Specialized Rooms

### Music Room
**Identifier:** ENV_School_006

**Teacher:** Musician Parrot (Melody Macaw) and Teacher Owl

**Description:** A vibrant room with warm orange walls and soundproofing panels painted with music notes. The room is designed to be lively and acoustically friendly.

**Features:**
- A collection of child-sized instruments: xylophones, tambourines, drums, maracas, triangles, bells, recorders, ukuleles.
- A small piano in the corner (painted with rainbow keys).
- A rug with a musical staff pattern where children sit.
- A mirror wall for watching movements during music and movement activities.
- A "Rhythm of the Week" board.
- A listening station with headphones and children's music albums.
- Storage cubbies labeled with instrument pictures.

**Activities:** Daily sing-alongs, rhythm games, instrument exploration, movement to music. Daisy Duck's favorite room.

### Art Room
**Identifier:** ENV_School_007

**Teacher:** Teacher Owl (art rotation) / Cat (Kitty) assists

**Description:** A bright, messy-friendly room with a washable tile floor and walls covered in inspiring artwork. Multiple easels face the garden windows.

**Features:**
- Six child-height easels with paint cups and brushes.
- A drying rack for artwork.
- A pottery wheel (child-safe, pedal-operated).
- A collage station with paper scraps, fabric, glue, scissors.
- A painting smock rack with colorful smocks.
- A "Masterpiece Wall" displaying weekly featured artwork.
- Sink with child-height counter for cleanup.
- Open shelving with art supplies in clear labeled bins.
- A spinning display carousel for postcards and art references.

**Natural Light:** Large north-facing windows provide consistent lighting. A skylight adds brightness.

**Characters:** Cat (Kitty) loves spending time here. Lily Bunny draws here. Elephant paints nature scenes.

### Gym
**Identifier:** ENV_School_008

**Description:** A large, bright open space with a high ceiling and polished wooden floor. One wall is covered floor-to-ceiling in mirrors. The opposite wall has climbing ropes and a rock-climbing wall (low, padded, child-safe).

**Features:**
- Basketball hoops at adjustable heights (currently at preschool level).
- Tumbling mats stored along the wall.
- A balance beam (low, 4 inches off ground).
- A parachute for group games (stored in ceiling bin).
- Cone markers and hula hoops in a storage closet.
- A scoreboard with friendly smiley faces instead of numbers.
- Padded floor squares in primary colors.
- An obstacle course kit.

**Activities:** Sports Day practice, dance class, free play, organized games.

**Characters:** Dog (Pup) and Horse (Dash) love the gym. Ben Bear enjoys obstacle courses. Daisy Duck practices dance here.

### School Playground
**Identifier:** ENV_School_009

**Description:** An outdoor playground adjacent to the gym, fenced with colorful plastic-coated chain link. The ground is soft rubberized mulch in rainbow colors.

**Features:**
- A main play structure with a small slide, a tunnel, and a bridge.
- Two baby swings and two regular swings.
- A small sandbox with a cover.
- A bouncy see-saw (spring-based, gentle).
- A tricycle path painted with traffic signs.
- Shade structure with a red and yellow striped canopy.
- Benches for teachers and parents.

**Characters:** Recess is enjoyed by all students. Monkey is the fastest on the tricycle path. Chicken organizes the games.

### Lunch Room / Cafeteria
**Identifier:** ENV_School_010

**Description:** A cheerful, noisy room with warm yellow walls and red-and-white checkered floor tiles. The ceiling has cloud-shaped light fixtures.

**Features:**
- Six round tables with four child-sized chairs each (primary colors).
- A serving counter at child-height with a sneeze guard.
- A daily menu board with illustrated food items.
- A "Star Table" where the quietest table gets to eat first.
- A hand-washing station with a step stool.
- A refrigerator for packed lunches and milk cartons.
- A bulletin board with nutrition facts and food group posters.
- A clean-up station with labeled bins (compost, recycle, trash).

**Menu Highlight:** Chef Pig and Mommy Bunny supply healthy, kid-friendly meals. Pizza Fridays are legendary.

**Characters:** Pig is always excited for lunch. Chef Pig visits to serve special meals. Doctor Panda checks the menu for nutritional balance.

### Science Room
**Identifier:** ENV_School_011

**Teacher:** Teacher Owl (Mr. Owl)

**Description:** A room designed for exploration and discovery, with pale blue walls and a tile floor (easy to clean). White countertops line the perimeter.

**Features:**
- A large central demonstration table with a sink.
- Microscopes (child-safe, sturdy) on the counters.
- A nature discovery table with magnifying glasses, tweezers, and specimen jars.
- A live terrarium with a friendly lizard.
- A small aquarium with fish.
- A weather station model on the wall.
- A "Science Word of the Week" board.
- Safety goggles hanging on hooks (child-sized).
- A collection of rocks, shells, and fossils in labeled trays.
- Plant growing station with small pots and soil.

**Activities:** Simple experiments (baking soda volcano, seed growing, sink/float), nature observation.

**Characters:** Teacher Owl leads science class. Elephant (Ellie) remembers every fact. Ben Bear loves experiments.

### School Library
**Identifier:** ENV_School_012

**Librarian:** Librarian Hedgehog (Miss Quills)

**Description:** A quiet, cozy room with warm cream walls and soft carpet. A large arched window with a window seat provides natural light.

**Features:**
- Low rounded bookshelves organized by color and reading level.
- A story time corner with a large cushion, a rocking chair, and a small puppet stage.
- Reading nooks — a tent, a beanbag corner, a window seat.
- A checkout desk shaped like an open book.
- A "Book of the Week" display stand.
- A listening station with audiobooks and headphones.
- A computer station with two child-friendly computers.
- A cozy rug with alphabet squares.
- Stuffed reading buddies (plush animals children can read to).

**Collections:** Picture books, early readers, non-fiction (animals, science, nature), audiobooks, parenting resources.

**Characters:** Librarian Hedgehog visits weekly for story time. Teacher Owl brings his class for book checkout. Lily Bunny is the most frequent borrower.

### Principal's Office
**Identifier:** ENV_School_013

**Occupant:** Principal Owl (Mr. Owl)

**Description:** The principal also serves as the head teacher, so this office serves dual purposes. A warm, welcoming room that is never intimidating.

**Features:**
- A rounded wooden desk with a nameplate: "Mr. Owl — Principal & Head Teacher."
- Two child-sized chairs across from the desk for student visitors.
- A cozy reading corner with an armchair and lamp.
- Bookshelves filled with educational books and resources.
- A "Good News" wall featuring student achievements.
- A jar of star stickers on the desk.
- A window overlooking the school garden.
- A bowl of apples on the side table (from Green Valley Farm).
- A framed photo of all students on the first day of school.

**Atmosphere:** Bright, warm, welcoming. No one is ever "sent to the principal's office" as punishment — it's where students go to share good news, get help, or have a quiet chat.

### Nurse's Office
**Identifier:** ENV_School_014

**Nurse:** Doctor Panda (Dr. Panda) visits weekly; a school nurse assistant is on staff daily.

**Description:** A soothing room with soft mint-green walls designed to calm anxious children. Medical equipment is child-friendly and non-threatening.

**Features:**
- A padded examination table with animal-print paper roll.
- A child-friendly scale (shaped like a frog).
- A medicine cabinet (locked) with basic supplies.
- A bed with a soft blanket for resting students.
- A rocking chair for the nurse.
- A "Brave Kid" sticker chart on the wall.
- A sink and counter for basic medical care.
- A refrigerator for medications and cold packs.
- A poster of "The Five Senses" and "How to Wash Your Hands."
- A small fish tank with calming fish.

**Supplies:** Bandages with cartoon characters, a toy stethoscope for play, ice packs, tissues, a first aid kit.

**Characters:** Doctor Panda visits to conduct health screenings. Children come for band-aids, tummy aches, and resting.

### School Garden
**Identifier:** ENV_School_015

**Description:** A large garden plot adjacent to the school building, visible from the classroom windows. Fenced with a low white picket fence.

**Features:**
- Raised garden beds at child-height, each labeled with a class name.
- Vegetables: carrots, tomatoes, lettuce, peas, beans, pumpkins.
- A compost bin painted with worms.
- A rainwater collection barrel (decorated with raindrop art).
- A tool shed with child-sized gardening tools.
- A scarecrow named "Professor Scarecrow" with glasses and a bow tie.
- A small greenhouse for seedling starting.
- A sundial in the center of the garden.
- Flower borders around the fence.
- A bench for garden observation and sketching.

**Characters:** Grandpa Bunny volunteers here. Cow (Cali) helps with watering. Lily Bunny and Ben Bear have their own plots. Teacher Owl uses the garden for science lessons.

### Outdoor Classroom
**Identifier:** ENV_School_016

**Description:** A shaded circular area with log benches arranged in a semicircle. A large tree stump serves as the teacher's podium. A chalkboard easel stands beside the stump.

**Features:**
- Six log benches (smooth, sealed, child-sized).
- A large oak tree providing natural shade.
- A portable whiteboard/chalkboard combo.
- A small weather station (thermometer, wind sock, rain gauge).
- A nature table with found objects (pinecones, leaves, rocks).
- String lights in the branches for late-afternoon lessons.
- A bell on a post (rung for outdoor class start/end).
- Flowering shrubs surrounding the area.

**Activities:** Nature study, story time, art class, science observation, music class.

**Characters:** Teacher Owl leads outdoor lessons. Musician Parrot holds outdoor concerts here. All students attend weekly.

### Preschool/Daycare Wing
**Identifier:** ENV_School_017

**Description:** A separate wing connected to the main building by a covered walkway. Designed for the youngest learners (ages 2-3), including Baby Bunny's age group.

**Exterior:** A smaller replica of the main building in softer colors (pastel pink, baby blue, mint green). A low fence encloses a dedicated outdoor play area.

**Interior Rooms:**

- **Toddler Classroom:** Soft padded floor in pastel colors. Low shelves with board books and soft blocks. A nap area with cribs and mats. Changing table. A low water/sensory play table.
- **Outdoor Toddler Play Area:** A small fenced yard with a mini slide (2ft tall), a sand table, push toys, and a shaded seating area for teachers.
- **Baby Room:** For infants (under 1 year), though this is rarely used in the show. Soft mats, mobiles, cribs, rocking chairs.
- **Staff Room:** For daycare teachers with a small kitchenette and scheduling board.

**Characters:** Baby Bunny attends the Toddler Classroom. Mommy Bunny drops him off before heading to the bakery. Lily walks him to his classroom before going to her own.
