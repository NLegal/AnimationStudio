# Vehicle Library Index — Little Learning Town

## Introduction

The Vehicle Library catalogs every mode of transportation in Little Learning Town. Vehicles are designed to feel like friendly characters themselves — with rounded bodies, smiling front grilles, bright cheerful colors, and oversized windows that make them approachable for young children.

Inspired by the Cocomelon aesthetic, every vehicle uses:
- **Rounded, bulbous shapes** — no sharp aerodynamic edges
- **Friendly front faces** — headlights as eyes, grille as a smile (where appropriate)
- **Bright primary and secondary colors** — red, blue, yellow, green, pink
- **Oversized windows** — so you can always see the passengers inside
- **Soft shading** — no harsh metallic reflections or realistic wear

## Quality Standards

- Matches the world's visual style (Cocomelon-inspired, Pixar-quality)
- Rounded corners and curved body panels throughout
- Bright, warm color palettes — no dark or military colors
- Friendly front-facing design (headlights look like eyes, grilles look like smiles)
- Proper scale relative to child characters (vehicles are slightly smaller than real-world counterparts to keep focus on characters)
- All wheels are oversized and rounded with visible hubcaps
- No text, logos, or branding on vehicle surfaces
- Suitable for both static scenes and simple animation (wheels can roll, propellers can spin)

## VEH_Land_001 — Family Car
