# Weather Guide

## Design Philosophy

Weather in Little Learning Town should always be **child-friendly and non-threatening**. No violent storms, dangerous lightning, or frightening conditions. Even rain and snow feel cozy and fun. Weather is an opportunity for visual variety and new activities.

---

## Sunny

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Description** | A bright, clear day with blue skies and warm sunlight. The most common weather setting. Everything looks vibrant, cheerful, and full of life. Shadows are crisp and well-defined. |
| **Visual Elements** | Clear blue sky, bright sun, soft white clouds (optional), warm light, green grass, flowers blooming |
| **Mood** | Cheerful, energetic, happy, bright, optimistic, playful |
| **Prompt Suffix** | `sunny weather, bright clear blue sky, warm sunlight, cheerful bright day, vibrant colors, sunny atmosphere, clear skies, warm and bright, happy weather, golden sunshine` |
| **Negative Prompt Additions** | `clouds, rain, dark sky, gloomy, overcast, shadows too dark` |

---

## Cloudy

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Description** | Soft clouds cover the sky, creating gentle diffused light. No harsh shadows. Everything looks soft, even, and calm. Colors are muted but still pleasant. A gentle, peaceful day. |
| **Visual Elements** | White/gray cloud cover, soft diffused light, gentle sky, minimal shadows, calm atmosphere |
| **Mood** | Calm, soft, gentle, peaceful, quiet, cozy, relaxed |
| **Prompt Suffix** | `cloudy weather, soft overcast sky, diffused gentle light, no harsh shadows, calm peaceful atmosphere, soft muted colors, gentle cloud cover, quiet cozy day, even soft lighting` |
| **Negative Prompt Additions** | `harsh shadows, bright sunlight, dark storm clouds, gloomy, depressing, dark gray` |

---

## Rain

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Description** | Gentle, friendly rain. Light to moderate showers. Puddles form on the ground. Umbrellas pop open. Everything looks clean and fresh. Rain is fun, not scary. Cozy to watch from inside. |
| **Visual Elements** | Falling rain streaks, puddles on ground, raindrops on windows, umbrellas, raincoats, wet shiny streets, clouds above |
| **Mood** | Cozy, fresh, clean, peaceful, playful, snuggly |
| **Prompt Suffix** | `gentle rain weather, soft falling rain, puddles on ground, umbrellas colorful, cozy rainy day, fresh clean atmosphere, raindrops on windows, wet shiny streets, playful rain, cozy inside watching rain` |
| **Negative Prompt Additions** | `heavy storm, flooding, lightning, thunder, dark scary clouds, dangerous weather, wind storm, hail` |

---

## Snow

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Description** | A winter wonderland. Soft, fluffy snow falling gently. Everything is covered in a white blanket. Buildings have snow caps. Quiet and magical. Perfect for play and holiday scenes. |
| **Visual Elements** | Falling snowflakes, snow-covered ground, snow on rooftops, snowmen, footprints in snow, frost on windows, mittens and scarves |
| **Mood** | Magical, quiet, peaceful, joyful, festive, wonderous, playful |
| **Prompt Suffix** | `snowy weather, gentle falling snow, white snow blanket, snow covered rooftops, magical winter wonderland, quiet peaceful snow, fluffy white snow, frosty windows, snow play, cozy winter magic` |
| **Negative Prompt Additions** | `dirty snow, slush, ice storm, blizzard, freezing, dark sky, dangerous cold, bare depressing trees` |

---

## Fog / Mist

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Description** | A light, gentle fog that softens the world. Not scary or thick. Think of a cozy misty morning. Shapes are soft, colors are muted, and everything feels quiet and mysterious in a friendly way. |
| **Visual Elements** | Low mist near ground, soft haze, diffused light, gentle atmosphere, distant objects fading softly, dew on grass |
| **Mood** | Mysterious (friendly), quiet, soft, dreamy, calm, magical |
| **Prompt Suffix** | `light fog, gentle mist, soft hazy atmosphere, dreamy foggy morning, mysterious but friendly, soft muted colors, gentle haze, magical misty day, quiet foggy atmosphere, soft dreamy weather` |
| **Negative Prompt Additions** | `thick fog, scary fog, horror fog, apocalypse, dark, suffocating, dense smoke, pollution, smog` |

---

## Wind

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Description** | A playful, breezy day. Leaves rustle and blow through the air. Hair moves gently. Kites fly high. Flags wave. Pinwheels spin. The wind is fun and energetic, never destructive. |
| **Visual Elements** | Leaves blowing, grass swaying, hair flowing, kites flying, flags waving, trees bending gently, wind lines, pinwheels spinning |
| **Mood** | Playful, energetic, lively, fun, breezy, carefree |
| **Prompt Suffix** | `windy weather, playful breeze, leaves blowing in wind, kites flying high, hair flowing gently, flags waving, trees swaying, energetic playful day, breezy atmosphere, wind playing, lively fun weather` |
| **Negative Prompt Additions** | `destructive wind, tornado, hurricane, flying debris, dangerous, storm damage, broken branches` |

## Rainbow

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Description** | A beautiful, colorful rainbow arching across the sky after rain. A symbol of hope and wonder. The world is fresh and clean. Colors are extra vibrant. Pure joy and magic. |
| **Visual Elements** | Bright rainbow arch, seven colors visible, soft remaining clouds, fresh wet ground, sun breaking through, colorful sky |
| **Mood** | Joyful, hopeful, magical, wonderous, happy, colorful, optimistic |
| **Prompt Suffix** | `rainbow in the sky, colorful arch, after rain freshness, bright rainbow colors, hopeful magical sky, sun breaking through clouds, joyful rainbow, vibrant colorful sky, weather clearing, beautiful rainbow light` |
| **Negative Prompt Additions** | `dark sky, storm, gloomy, double rainbow (unless wanted), gray, dull colors` |

---

## Light Storm

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Description** | Distant thunder and gentle clouds. Safe to watch from a window. The storm is far away — just interesting enough to be cozy. Maybe a few flashes of distant lightning. No danger, just atmosphere. |
| **Visual Elements** | Distant dark clouds, soft thunder flashes, gentle rain possible, cozy indoor view, window framing, warm light inside |
| **Mood** | Cozy, safe, snug, calm, interesting but not scary, warm inside contrast |
| **Prompt Suffix** | `light storm in distance, cozy inside watching storm, distant thunder, safe warm indoors, gentle storm atmosphere, far away lightning flashes, dark clouds far away, cozy snug weather, storm watching, warm safe inside` |
| **Negative Prompt Additions** | `close lightning, dangerous storm, tornado, hurricane, hail, damaging wind, flooding, scary thunder, power outage` |
