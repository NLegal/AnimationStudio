# Time-of-Day Lighting Guide

## Design Philosophy

All lighting should feel **safe, warm, and inviting**. Even night scenes should be cozy and magical — never dark, scary, or gloomy. Use diffused, colorful light that wraps subjects in a friendly glow.

---

## Sunrise

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Description** | The sun just peeking over the horizon. Soft, warm light with gentle rays spreading across the sky. Long soft shadows stretch away from objects. Everything is quiet and fresh. |
| **Color Palette** | Warm orange, peach, pale pink, soft lavender sky, golden-yellow highlights |
| **Mood** | Peaceful, hopeful, fresh start, quiet, gentle awakening |
| **Prompt Suffix** | `sunrise lighting, warm orange and pink sky, long soft shadows, golden sun rays, gentle glow, fresh morning, mist rising, peaceful atmosphere, cinematic lighting, soft warm tones, pastel dawn sky` |
| **Best Used For** | Opening scenes, morning routines, new beginnings, nature walks, school arrival |

---

## Morning

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Description** | Bright, clean light with the sun fully risen. Golden tones that feel energetic but not harsh. Shadows are moderate and well-defined. The world looks fresh and ready for the day. |
| **Color Palette** | Bright golden, warm yellow, clear blue sky, fresh green, white highlights |
| **Mood** | Energetic, clean, fresh, optimistic, active, cheerful |
| **Prompt Suffix** | `morning lighting, bright golden sunlight, clean fresh atmosphere, cheerful bright day, blue sky with soft clouds, golden highlights, energetic mood, vibrant colors, warm sunlight, crisp clean look` |
| **Best Used For** | Daily activities, playground scenes, outdoor adventures, school lessons, arrival scenes |

---

## Noon

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Description** | Bright white overhead light. Minimal shadows directly under objects. High contrast but softened for a child-friendly look. Colors appear vibrant and fully saturated. Everything is clearly visible. |
| **Color Palette** | Bright white, deep blue sky, fully saturated colors, crisp shadows, bright green |
| **Mood** | Energetic, lively, clear, vibrant, active, bold |
| **Prompt Suffix** | `noon lighting, bright overhead sun, minimal shadows, high-key lighting, vibrant saturated colors, clear blue sky, bright white highlights, crisp clean, lively atmosphere, summer brightness` |
| **Best Used For** | Outdoor play, beach scenes, sports activities, picnic scenes, market scenes |

---
