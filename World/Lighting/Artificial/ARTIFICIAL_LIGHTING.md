# Artificial Lighting Guide

## Design Philosophy

Artificial lights in Little Learning Town should always feel **inviting and warm**. Even "cool" lighting should never feel harsh, clinical, or uninviting. Light sources are opportunities to add visual interest and coziness.

---

## Warm Indoor Lighting

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Description** | The standard indoor lighting for homes. Warm, soft light from lamps, ceiling fixtures, and fireplaces. Creates a cozy, lived-in feel. Light pools in specific areas with soft falloff. |
| **Color Temperature** | Warm — 2700K–3200K (amber to soft yellow) |
| **Mood** | Cozy, homey, safe, comfortable, inviting, warm |
| **Prompt Suffix** | `warm indoor lighting, cozy lamp glow, soft golden light, warm amber tones, homey atmosphere, comfortable indoor lighting, soft shadows, inviting warm space, gentle yellow light, lived-in glow` |
| **Best Used For** | Living rooms, bedrooms, kitchens, dinner scenes, family time, reading nooks |

---

## Cool Indoor Lighting

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Description** | Brighter, cleaner light for task-oriented spaces. Not cold or clinical — think bright, clean, and focused. Used in kitchens, bathrooms, and workspaces where visibility matters. |
| **Color Temperature** | Neutral-cool — 4000K–5000K (clean white with slight blue) |
| **Mood** | Clean, focused, bright, fresh, alert, organized |
| **Prompt Suffix** | `cool indoor lighting, bright clean white light, fresh atmosphere, clear bright space, task lighting, clean modern feel, crisp white tones, organized bright room, alert focused mood, fresh indoor light` |
| **Best Used For** | Bathrooms, kitchens during cooking, art areas, science experiments, classroom activities |

---

## Fairy / String Lights

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Description** | Delicate tiny lights strung along walls, ceilings, trees, or fences. Each light is a small warm or colored dot. Creates a magical, twinkling atmosphere. Perfect for bedrooms and outdoor evening spaces. |
| **Color Temperature** | Warm white or multicolor — 2700K (warm) or RGB |
| **Mood** | Magical, whimsical, cozy, dreamy, festive, enchanting |
| **Prompt Suffix** | `fairy string lights, twinkling warm lights, magical glowing dots, whimsical atmosphere, cozy twinkle, enchanting light strings, dreamy sparkle, warm fairy lights, magical evening glow, soft colorful lights` |
| **Best Used For** | Bedroom decor, garden parties, evening picnics, treehouses, holiday decor, magical hideouts |

---

## Holiday Lights

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Description** | Bright, colorful lights strung on houses, trees, and streets. Bold reds, greens, blues, golds, and whites. Often blinking or twinkling. Creates instant festive cheer. |
| **Color Temperature** | Multicolor bold — warm and cool mixed (RGB + warm white) |
| **Mood** | Festive, joyful, exciting, cheerful, celebratory, bright |
| **Prompt Suffix** | `holiday lights, colorful festive lighting, bright red and green lights, twinkling decorations, warm glowing bulbs, cheerful holiday atmosphere, decorated with lights, festive sparkle, joyful colorful glow, celebration lights` |
| **Best Used For** | Christmas scenes, holiday specials, winter festivals, town square decorations, party scenes |

---

## Stage / Spot Lighting

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Description** | Focused, directed light for performances and presentations. A single bright pool of light on stage with darker surroundings. Can be warm or cool depending on the scene. Dramatic but not harsh. |
| **Color Temperature** | Variable — 3200K (warm stage) to 5600K (bright spot) |
| **Mood** | Dramatic, focused, exciting, special, important, celebratory |
| **Prompt Suffix** | `stage spotlight, focused dramatic lighting, pool of warm light, performer highlighted, theatrical atmosphere, bright center stage, dramatic shadows around edges, special moment lighting, performance focus warm light` |
| **Best Used For** | School plays, talent shows, concerts, presentations, dance performances, show-and-tell |

---

## Candle Light

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Description** | The warmest, most intimate artificial light. A small flickering flame casting soft, dancing shadows. Everything glows with a deep amber-orange. Feels sacred, quiet, and special. |
| **Color Temperature** | Very warm — 1800K–2200K (deep amber to orange) |
| **Mood** | Intimate, calm, peaceful, special, quiet, reflective, cozy |
| **Prompt Suffix** | `candlelight, warm flickering flame, intimate amber glow, soft dancing shadows, quiet cozy atmosphere, warm orange light, peaceful intimate setting, soft candle glow, gentle flicker, calm special moment` |
| **Best Used For** | Birthday cakes, bedtime, power outages, quiet talks, holiday dinners, bath time, prayer/meditation |
