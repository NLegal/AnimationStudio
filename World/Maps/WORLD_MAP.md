## Scale & Orientation

- **Orientation:** North is up. Pine Mountain is north. Sandy Cove is south.
- **Map scale:** The town spans approximately 3 miles (north to south) and 4 miles (east to west). Walking from Sunny Meadow to Main Street takes about 10 minutes. A bicycle ride from the Academy to the Beach takes 15 minutes.
- **Elevation:** Pine Mountain summit at 2,227 ft. Town elevation averages 200–400 ft above sea level. Sandy Cove is at sea level.
- **Sun path:** The sun rises over Green Valley Farm (east) and sets beyond Whispering Woods (west).

## Regional Connections

### Sunny Meadow → Main Street
**Connection:** Meadow Lane (main road, 0.5 miles)
Sunny Meadow's southern boundary feeds directly into the northern end of Main Street via a gentle downhill slope. A wide sidewalk lined with cherry blossom trees connects the residential zone to the downtown shopping district. Children can walk, bike, or scoot safely along this route.

### Main Street → Little Learning Academy
**Connection:** Academy Avenue (main road, 0.4 miles)
South of Main Street, Academy Avenue runs straight to the school's main gate. A traffic circle with a giant pencil sculpture marks the junction. Crossing guards (friendly animal characters) help children cross at peak hours.

### Main Street → Happy Hills Park
**Connection:** Park Boulevard (east road, 0.3 miles)
A short road leading east from the Town Square. The entrance to Happy Hills Park is marked by a giant rainbow arch with "Happy Hills Park" in colorful letters.

### Happy Hills Park → Sunny Meadow
**Connection:** Meadow Path (walking/bike path, 0.6 miles)
A winding path through a small wooded area connects the park's northwest exit to the southern edge of Sunny Meadow. Benches and water fountains are spaced along the route.

### Whispering Woods → Happy Hills Park
**Connection:** Forest Trail (hiking path, 0.8 miles)
A gentle dirt path emerges from the tree line at the southeast corner of Whispering Woods and enters Happy Hills Park's northwest corner. A wooden sign at the junction reads "Whispering Woods →" with a friendly owl carving.

### Whispering Woods → Green Valley Farm
**Connection:** Farm Road (paved road, 1.2 miles)
A scenic road skirts the northern edge of the woods and crosses a stone bridge over the Crystal River. The road continues east into the farm's western entrance past the windmill.

### Green Valley Farm → Happy Hills Park
**Connection:** Country Lane (paved road, 1.0 mile)
A straight, flat road running south from the farm's main gate to the park's eastern entrance. Hay bales decorate the roadside during autumn.

### Sunny Meadow → Green Valley Farm
**Connection:** Sunrise Drive (main road, 1.5 miles)
A longer route passing through the eastern edge of Sunny Meadow and curving northeast into the farm. This road offers views of both the mountains and the ocean in the distance.

### Little Learning Academy → Sandy Cove
**Connection:** Shore Road (main road, 0.7 miles)
A gentle southward road with a slight curve. Halfway down, a lookout point called "Cove Vista" offers the first glimpse of the ocean. The road ends at the beach parking area.

### Sandy Cove → Main Street
**Connection:** Harbor Lane (coastal road, 0.9 miles)
A scenic coastal road hugging the shore before curving northwest back into Main Street's southern entrance. The lighthouse is visible for the entire journey.

### Whispering Woods → Dreamland
**Connection:** Rainbow Bridge / Enchanted Path
Dreamland has no permanent physical road. It is reached via the **Rainbow Bridge** — a shimmering, translucent arch that appears after rain showers, located deep within Whispering Woods. Alternatively, the **Enchanted Path** (marked by glowing mushrooms) becomes visible at twilight, winding deeper into the forest until the landscape transforms into Dreamland. These routes are not shown on standard maps, as they appear only under special conditions.

### Pine Mountain Access
**Connection:** Mountain Road (paved, winding, 2.5 miles from Sunny Meadow)
The only vehicle-accessible route begins at the north end of Sunny Meadow, climbing gently in a series of switchbacks through pine forest. A cable car also runs from the Mountain Base Trailhead to the Summit Meadow, suitable for young children and strollers.

## Navigation Notes

### Walking Routes for Episodes
1. **Morning Walk to School:** Start at ENV_Residential_001 → walk south on Meadow Lane → cross Town Square → continue on Academy Avenue → arrive at ENV_School_001
2. **Park Adventure:** Start at ENV_Residential_004 (neighborhood park) → take Meadow Path → enter Happy Hills Park → reach ENV_Playground_001
3. **Farm Visit:** Start at ENV_Downtown_003 (Bakery) → take Park Boulevard → turn east on Country Lane → arrive at ENV_Farm_001
4. **Forest Exploration:** Start at ENV_Forest_001 → follow Wandering Path → reach ENV_Forest_004 (Waterfall Pool)
5. **Beach Day:** Start at ENV_School_001 → take Shore Road → arrive at ENV_Beach_001
6. **Mountain Trip:** Start at Sunny Meadow → take Mountain Road → stop at ENV_Mountains_003 (Lookout Point) → continue to ENV_Mountains_004 (Summit)
7. **Dreamland Journey (special):** Start at ENV_Forest_001 → reach Rainbow Bridge (ENV_Fantasy_009) after rain → enter ENV_Fantasy_002 (Cloud Village)

### Vehicle Routes for Episodes
1. **School Bus Route:** ENV_Residential stops → Main Street → ENV_School_001 (VEH_Bus_001)
2. **Fire Truck Emergency:** ENV_Downtown_011 → any zone (VEH_FireTruck_001)
3. **Family Drive:** ENV_Residential_001 → ENV_Beach_001 via Sunrise Drive & Shore Road

## Environmental Notes

- **Seasonal transitions** affect path accessibility. Mountain Road has light snow in winter. Forest Trail may have fallen leaves in autumn. Shore Road is always clear.
- **The Crystal River** flows from Pine Mountain (ENV_Mountains_007) through Whispering Woods (ENV_Forest_003), past Green Valley Farm (ENV_Farm_010 pond), and empties into the ocean at Sandy Cove's eastern edge.
- **The train line** runs along the eastern edge of town, connecting the Main Street Depot to the Farm Station and the Mountain Base Station. It does not run through Whispering Woods or Dreamland.
- **Bike lanes** exist on all main roads. Bike parking is available at every major landmark.
- **Street lamps** are designed as giant lollipops or dandelions, emitting a warm golden glow at night.
- **All signs** use the town's rounded sans-serif font with child-friendly icons.
