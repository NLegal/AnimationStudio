## Overview

Sunny Meadow is the heart of Little Learning Town, a gently rolling neighborhood of rounded cottages with colorful front doors, picket fences, and perfectly manicured lawns. Every house has a garden bursting with flowers, a driveway with a family car, and a mailbox painted with the family's name. Winding sidewalks connect the homes, shaded by large friendly trees with low-hanging branches perfect for climbing. Small neighborhood parks dot the area with benches, birdbaths, and community vegetable gardens. The streets are quiet and safe, with painted crosswalks and oversized street signs that help children learn colors and shapes.

**Color Palette:** Soft yellows (#FFF5D6), warm greens (#C3D9B2), buttercream, peach (#FFDAB9), lavender

**Design Notes:** Everything is child-scale (30% smaller than real-world). Doors are 4ft tall. Windows start at 2ft from ground. Fences have rounded pickets. No sharp edges anywhere.

## Community Spaces

### Neighborhood Park (Sunny Meadow Park)
**Identifier:** ENV_Residential_016

**Location:** Corner of Sunny Lane and Meadow Drive

**Description:** A small neighborhood park with a grassy field, a central gazebo with curved benches, and a small playground with a slide and two swings (preschooler-sized). Oversized flower beds with seasonal blooms. A community bulletin board shaped like a tree.

- A garden chess set (large pieces, child-safe).

### Community Garden
**Identifier:** ENV_Residential_017

**Location:** End of Honeysuckle Lane

**Description:** A shared garden space divided into plots. Each family has a named plot with colorful signs. Raised beds at child-height. A central compost area. A tool shed (shared). A rainwater collection barrel painted with flowers.

**Notable Features:**
- Grandpa Bunny's prize-winning tomato plot.
- A scarecrow named "Friendly Fred" with a smiling face.
- A community herb spiral.
- A small greenhouse for seedlings.
- A bench with a view of the entire garden.

### Tree-Lined Walking Path
**Identifier:** ENV_Residential_018

**Description:** A winding paved path that connects the residential streets. Lined with alternating cherry blossom trees and maple trees. Benches every 100ft. Solar-powered lanterns shaped like dandelions light the path at night.

**Notable Features:**
- A small wooden bridge over a decorative creek.
- Wildflower patches along the edges.
- Birdhouses on every fifth tree.
- Distance markers in kid-friendly units ("100 hops from here").
- A small lending library (a birdhouse-shaped book exchange).

### Mailbox Row

### Street Lamps (Residential Variant)
**Identifier:** ENV_Residential_020

**Description:** Street lamps designed as giant dandelions on curved stems. They emit a warm golden glow at night. The "seed head" of the dandelion is the light source. Spaced every 50ft along all streets.

### Sidewalks & Crosswalks
**Identifier:** ENV_Residential_021

**Description:** Wide smooth sidewalks in warm cream-colored paving. Crosswalks are painted in rainbow colors at every intersection. Oversized street signs help children learn — the sign at Sunny Lane & Meadow Drive reads "Sunny Lane" in yellow on a green background with a sun icon and "Meadow Drive" in pink with a flower icon.

### Street Signs
**Identifier:** ENV_Residential_022

**Description:** Friendly, oversized signs with rounded corners. Each sign includes both the street name and a small icon representing the street's theme (sun for Sunny Lane, flower for Meadow Drive, butterfly for Buttercup Way, etc.). The posts are short (child-height) so kids can read them.

### Bus Stop (Residential)
**Identifier:** ENV_Residential_023

**Location:** Sunny Lane & Meadow Drive

**Description:** A covered bus stop with a rounded yellow roof and a bench. A bus schedule posted at child-height. A small sign showing the Green Line route. The Town Bus (VEH_Bus_001) stops here four times daily.

### Recycling & Trash Collection Point
**Identifier:** ENV_Residential_024

**Description:** Color-coded bins at each home — blue for recycling, green for compost, gray for trash. Bins are rounded and bear-shaped (decorative). The collection truck visits every Tuesday morning.

### Welcome Sign
**Identifier:** ENV_Residential_025

**Location:** Entry point from Meadow Lane into Sunny Meadow

**Description:** A large, curved wooden sign reading "Welcome to Sunny Meadow" surrounded by flower beds. The letters are painted in multiple bright colors. A small bunny silhouette is carved at the bottom.
