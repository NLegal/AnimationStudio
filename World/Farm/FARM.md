**Production:** AI Nursery Rhyme Studio

---

## Overview

Green Valley Farm sits on the eastern edge of Little Learning Town, a picturesque landscape of rolling green hills, red barns with white trim, and fields stretching toward the horizon. The farm is home to friendly animals who are also characters in the town's world. A windmill stands tall beside the pond, its blades spinning gently in the breeze. Children can visit to learn about where food comes from, meet the animals, and help with simple farm tasks. The farm is both a working agricultural site and a learning environment.

**Color Palette:** Barn red (#8B0000), hay yellow (#E8C547), grass green (#7CFC00), sky blue, wheat gold

**Design Notes:** Every structure is painted and well-maintained. No rust, no decay, no dangerous equipment exposed. Animals are friendly and approachable. Fences are smooth, rounded, and safe. The farm feels alive and productive, never abandoned or run-down.

## Main Barn
**Identifier:** ENV_Farm_001

### Exterior

**Architecture:** A classic red barn with white trim and a gambrel roof in dark brown. The barn is large and welcoming, with a wide central door (always open during the day) revealing the hay-strewn interior.

**Features:**
- A weather vane shaped like a rooster on the peak.
- A hayloft door on the upper level, sometimes open with hay spilling out.
- A white cupola with louvers on the roof ridge.
- Red-painted wooden siding with white corner boards.
- A large sliding door on the front (red, with a star decoration).
- A smaller personnel door beside the main door.
- A barn quilt pattern painted on the upper facade (a nine-patch in red and cream).
- Flower boxes under the hayloft window.
- Swallows' nests under the eaves.

**Surroundings:**
- A gravel path leads to the barn from the farm lane.
- An old wooden bench beside the door.
- A water pump (decorative, functional) near the entrance.
- Lantern-style light on a post by the door.

### Interior

**Identifier:** ENV_Farm_001a

**Description:** The barn interior is warm, dusty with hay, and filled with golden light streaming through the windows and gaps in the siding. The floor is packed earth mixed with hay. The ceiling soars to the roof peak.

**Main Floor:**
- **Central Aisle:** Wide enough for a tractor, lined with stalls on both sides.
- **Tack Room:** A small room with saddles, bridles, and horse care supplies hanging on wooden pegs.
- **Feed Storage:** Bins of grain, bags of feed, mineral blocks.
- **Tool Wall:** Rakes, pitchforks, shovels, and brooms hanging on labeled hooks.
- **Milking Station:** A clean, tiled area with a milking stool and buckets (for Cow).
- **Water Trough:** A large galvanized steel trough with fresh water.

**Hayloft:**
- Accessed by a wooden ladder with wide rungs.
- Bales of hay stacked neatly, with a small fort-like space built into the stacks.
- A large window looking out over the fields.
- A pulley system for lifting hay bales.
- A swing rope hanging from the loft beam (for play, supervised).

**Atmosphere:** Warm, golden, dusty, smelling of hay and animal. Sunbeams cut through the dim interior. Friendly and inviting.

**Characters:** Farmer Goat (Farmer Billy) works here daily. Cow (Cali) is milked here. Horse (Dash) is stabled here in bad weather. Children visit for barn tours.

## Corn Field
**Identifier:** ENV_Farm_007

**Description:** A large field of corn stalks stretching in neat rows. The field is approximately 2 acres, bordered by wildflowers and a wooden fence.

**Features:**
- Corn stalks grow 6-8ft tall (child-height + a bit).
- A dirt path runs between rows for exploring.
- A corn maze is cut into the field each autumn.
- Scarecrows at each corner.
- A small irrigation pump at the edge.
- Field mice (friendly background characters) live at the edges.

**Seasonal:**
- **Spring:** Young green shoots.
- **Summer:** Tall green stalks, tassels forming.
- **Autumn:** Golden stalks, harvest, corn maze.
- **Winter:** Cut stalks, bare field, resting.

**Activities:** Corn maze in autumn, learning about plant growth, harvest.

## Windmill
**Identifier:** ENV_Farm_010

**Description:** A classic Dutch-style windmill standing beside the farm pond. The windmill is painted white with a red cap and dark wood trim. The blades turn gently in the breeze.

**Structure:**
- **Base:** White stucco with a small door and two round windows.
- **Mid-Section:** Tapered, with a wooden observation platform and railing.
- **Cap:** Red, conical, with the blades attached.
- **Blades:** Four, with wooden lattice frames, always turning slowly.

**Function:** The windmill powers a small water pump that circulates water from the pond through the farm's irrigation system. It is also a decorative landmark visible from the road.

**Features:**
- A small bench at the base for enjoying the view.
- Flower beds around the foundation.
- A wooden sign explaining how windmills work (educational).
- The interior contains the grinding mechanism (visible through a glass panel).
- A spiral staircase inside (safe, with handrails).

**Characters:** Farmer Goat checks the windmill daily. Teacher Owl brings students for lessons about renewable energy.
