# Phase 2 — World Building & Environment Bible

## AI Nursery Studio

### Version 1.0

---

# Objective

With the characters complete, the next step is to build their world.

Do **not** create stories yet.

Do **not** animate.

Do **not** generate songs.

Your goal is to build a reusable, modular world that every episode can use.

Think of it like building a movie studio backlot.

Once the town, houses, school, farm, and park exist, they can be reused indefinitely with different characters and stories.

---

# End Goal

At the end of Phase 2 you should have:

* Complete town map
* Named locations
* Environment style guide
* Interior and exterior sets
* Seasonal variants
* Time-of-day variants
* Weather variants
* Modular props
* Navigation map
* Color palette
* Camera reference library
* Prompt templates
* Environment reference sheets

Every future video should reuse these assets.

---

# Design Philosophy

The world should feel:

* Safe
* Bright
* Warm
* Colorful
* Educational
* Curious
* Friendly
* Clean
* Optimistic

Children should instantly recognize the world after only a few episodes.

---

# World Rules

Everything follows the same design language.

No realistic cities.

No abandoned buildings.

No graffiti.

No broken roads.

No dangerous environments.

Everything should appear inviting and safe.

---

# The World

Name your world.

Example

Sunny Meadows

Happy Hills

Dream Forest

A named world strengthens your brand.

# Deliverables

At the completion of Phase 2 you should have:

* Complete world map with named regions
* 30–50 permanent environments
* Interior and exterior reference sets
* Seasonal, weather, and lighting variants
* Modular prop library
* Vehicle library
* Camera-angle reference library
* Environment prompt templates
* Negative prompt templates
* Organized world asset repository

With Phases 1 and 2 complete, your studio now owns a persistent universe: recognizable characters living in recognizable places. Every future nursery rhyme, educational lesson, or holiday special can be created by reusing and recombining these assets instead of starting from scratch, dramatically improving consistency, production speed, and brand identity.

You now have the two most valuable long-term assets of an animation studio: **who** lives in your world (Phase 1) and **where** they live (Phase 2). The next logical phase would be **Phase 3 – Asset & Object Library**, covering every reusable object (toys, food, furniture, instruments, animals, classroom materials, seasonal decorations, etc.) that characters interact with across episodes. This is where production speed increases dramatically because most scenes become compositions of existing assets rather than entirely new creations.
