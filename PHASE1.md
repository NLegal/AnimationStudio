# Objective

Phase 1 is **the foundation of the entire studio**.

Do **NOT** create videos yet.

Do **NOT** generate songs.

Do **NOT** animate anything.

Your only goal is to build a **production-quality universe** that can be reused across thousands of videos.

Think like Pixar.

Before *Toy Story* existed, Pixar designed Woody.

Before *Frozen* existed, Disney designed Elsa.

We are doing the same thing.

# Art Direction

Choose one visual style and never change it.

Recommended

**Cocomelon-inspired**

Characteristics

* colorful
* rounded shapes
* oversized eyes
* soft lighting
* vibrant colors
* high saturation
* Pixar-quality rendering
* child friendly
* no sharp edges
* clean geometry
* smooth skin
* expressive faces

Never mix styles.

Do not suddenly generate anime.

Do not switch to watercolor.

Consistency builds a recognizable brand.

# Deliverables

At the completion of Phase 1 you should have:

* 10–20 finalized main and supporting characters
* Complete character bible
* Standardized prompt library
* Standardized negative prompts
* Model sheets and turnaround references
* Expression, pose, outfit, and accessory libraries
* Style guide and color palette
* Organized asset repository ready for image, animation, and video generation

No songs. No videos. No animation.

Only after this universe is complete should Phase 2 begin, where the focus shifts to designing the reusable environments, props, and locations that these characters will inhabit.
