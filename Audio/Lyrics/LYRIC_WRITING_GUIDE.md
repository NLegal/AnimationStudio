## Introduction

Lyrics for preschool content are the primary vehicle for learning. Every song must be musically engaging, linguistically accessible, and educationally purposeful.

The goal is not just to entertain but to teach — letters, numbers, colors, shapes, social skills, emotional regulation, and daily routines — all through lyrics that children can sing along to after a single listen.

---

## Song Structure

### Standard Structure

```text
[Intro]          → 4–8 bars, instrumental or humming
[Verse 1]        → 4–8 lines, introduces the concept
[Chorus]         → 4–8 lines, the hook (repeated identically)
[Verse 2]        → 4–8 lines, expands the concept
[Chorus]         → Same as first chorus
[Bridge]         → 4–8 lines, new perspective or twist
[Chorus]         → Final chorus (maybe with slight variation)
[Outro]          → 4–8 bars, fade or instrumental resolution
```

### Alternative Structures

| Structure | When to Use | Example |
|-----------|-------------|---------|
| Verse-Chorus-Verse-Chorus | Simple concepts, short attention | Color songs, counting songs |
| Call-and-Response only | Interactive songs | "If You're Happy and You Know It" style |
| Cumulative | Build on each verse | "Old MacDonald" style animal songs |
| Question-Answer | Teach through dialogue | "What color is the sky?" style |
| Story-song | Narrative-driven | Morning routine, social stories |

### Duration Guidelines

| Duration | Content Type | Max Lines |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| 30 seconds | Short intro, transition song | 8–12 lines |
| 60 seconds | Single concept song | 16–24 lines |
| 90 seconds | Standard educational song | 24–36 lines |
| 2 minutes | Full theme song | 32–48 lines |
| 3 minutes | Story-song, compilation | 48–64 lines |

## Rhyme Schemes

### AABB (Simple)

Best for youngest audiences (ages 2–3). Predictable and easy to memorize.

```
The sun is up, it's time to play     (A)
Let's have a happy, sunny day        (A)
We'll learn new things along the way (A)
And sing and dance and shout hooray  (A)
```

### ABAB (Classic)

Standard rhyme scheme for educational songs.

```
I see a bird up in the tree       (A)
It sings a song for you and me    (B)
It flaps its wings and flies so high (A)
Up to the clouds across the sky   (B)
```

### ABCB (Ballad)

Good for storytelling and emotional content.

```
The moon is shining bright tonight (A)
The stars begin to glow            (B)
I close my eyes and drift to sleep (C)
The world is soft and slow         (B)
```

### Internal Rhyme (Advanced)

Occasional use for more sophisticated songs (ages 4–6).

```
I found a round red ball, I watched it rise and fall
I bounced it down the hall against the wall
```

## Vocabulary Guidelines

### Preschool Word List

Use words from the following categories. Avoid abstract, multi-syllabic, or rarely used words.

| Category | Approved Words |
|----------|---------------|
| **Greetings** | hello, hi, goodbye, bye, good morning, good night |
| **Actions** | run, jump, clap, stomp, hop, skip, dance, sing, eat, drink, sleep, wake, wash, brush, clean, play, share, help, give, take, sit, stand, wave, hug, kiss, smile, laugh, cry |
| **Colors** | red, blue, yellow, green, orange, purple, pink, brown, black, white, gray, gold |
| **Numbers** | one, two, three, four, five, six, seven, eight, nine, ten, zero, hundred |
| **Shapes** | circle, square, triangle, rectangle, star, heart, diamond, oval, crescent |
| **Body** | head, shoulders, knees, toes, eyes, ears, mouth, nose, hands, fingers, feet, tummy |
| **Animals** | dog, cat, bird, fish, cow, horse, pig, sheep, duck, chicken, frog, rabbit, bear, lion, elephant, monkey, turtle, butterfly, bee, ant |
| **Food** | apple, banana, orange, grape, milk, water, bread, cheese, egg, rice, pasta, cookie, cake, ice cream, soup, carrot, pea, bean |
| **Family** | mama, dada, mommy, daddy, sister, brother, baby, grandma, grandpa, aunt, uncle |
| **Places** | home, school, park, zoo, farm, store, beach, pool, library, playground |
| **Emotions** | happy, sad, angry, scared, brave, silly, tired, hungry, thirsty, loved |
| **Weather** | sun, rain, cloud, snow, wind, rainbow, thunder, lightning, fog, warm, cold, hot |
| **Time** | morning, afternoon, evening, night, today, tomorrow, yesterday, now, later |
| **Nature** | tree, flower, grass, sky, ocean, river, mountain, star, moon, sun, rock, leaf |
| **Toys** | ball, doll, block, puzzle, book, crayon, paint, swing, slide, bike, train, drum |
| **Size** | big, small, tall, short, long, wide, tiny, huge |
| **Location** | up, down, in, out, on, off, over, under, inside, outside, here, there |
| **Quantity** | all, some, many, few, lots, more, less, none, every |
| **Qualities** | good, nice, kind, gentle, soft, hard, fast, slow, loud, quiet, clean, dirty, wet, dry |

### Vocabulary Rules

- Maximum 3 syllables per word (except character names)
- No idioms, metaphors, or figurative language
- No words requiring abstract reasoning
- Define new words with context in the same line
- Repeat new vocabulary 4–5 times in the song
- Use concrete nouns and action verbs

## Repetition Patterns

Repetition is the most powerful learning tool in preschool content.

### Chorus Repetition

| Song Length | Chorus Repeats |
|-------------|----------------|
| 30 seconds | 2 times |
| 60 seconds | 3 times |
| 90 seconds | 3–4 times |
| 2 minutes | 4 times |

### Keyword Repetition

- Key educational words: repeat 4–5 times per song
- New vocabulary: repeat in verse, then chorus, then bridge
- Questions: ask, repeat answer, ask again
- Actions: describe, demonstrate, repeat

### Phrase Repetition

```
Can you clap your hands with me?     (first ask)
Clap your hands along with me        (instruction)
Everybody clap your hands            (group action)
Clap, clap, clap your hands          (celebration)
```

## Educational Content Integration

### Natural Integration (Preferred)

```
Let's count the apples on the tree,
One, two, three, for you and me.
Four, five, six, as tall can be,
Seven, eight, nine, ten, we're free!
```

The counting happens naturally within a context (apples on a tree) rather than reciting numbers in isolation.

### Call-and-Response

```
Leader:  "What color is the sky today?"
Children: "Blue! Blue! Blue!"
Leader:  "What color is the grass, let's say?"
Children: "Green! Green! Green!"
```

### Action Integration

```
Stand up tall and touch the sky    (action: reach up)
Now bend down and touch your toes  (action: bend)
Wiggle your fingers, wink your eye (action: wiggle, wink)
See how many ways your body grows  (action: stretch wide)
```

### Alphabet Integration

```
A is for apple, red and sweet,
B is for ball that bounces down the street,
C is for cat who purrs all day,
D is for dog who loves to play.
```

### Counting Integration

```
One little bird sitting in a tree,
Two little birds singing songs for me,
Three little birds flying up so high,
Four little birds across the sky,
Five little birds — wave goodbye!
```

## Song Categories & Lyric Templates

### Alphabet Songs

Structure: Each letter gets 2 lines, chunked into groups of 3–4 letters per verse.

Vocabulary per letter:
- A: apple, ant, alligator
- B: ball, bird, butterfly, banana
- C: cat, car, cookie, cloud
- D: dog, duck, dinosaur, drum
- E: egg, elephant, eyes
- F: fish, flower, frog, friend
- G: goat, guitar, grape, garden
- H: hat, horse, heart, happy
- I: insect, igloo, ice cream
- J: jump, jelly, jigsaw, joy
- K: kite, kitten, key, kind
- L: lion, leaf, lamp, love
- M: monkey, moon, milk, mama
- N: nest, nose, night, noodles
- O: orange, octopus, ocean, owl
- P: pig, penguin, pizza, park
- Q: queen, quilt, quiet
- R: rabbit, rainbow, river, run
- S: sun, star, snake, swing
- T: tree, turtle, train, tomato
- U: umbrella, under, up
- V: violin, volcano, vegetable
- W: water, whale, window, wind
- X: xylophone, x-ray
- Y: yellow, yarn, yawn
- Z: zebra, zoo, zigzag

### Number Songs

Count objects in context. Use animals, fruit, toys, or fingers.

Counting by 2s:
```
Two little feet go stomp, stomp, stomp,
Four little hands go clap, clap, clap,
Six little fingers waving in the air,
Eight little toes — wiggle them there!
Ten little numbers — let's all share!
```

### Color Songs

Assign colors to familiar objects.

```
Red is the color of a big red ball,
Blue is the color of the sky so tall,
Yellow is the sun up in the sky,
Green is the grass that grows up high.
```

### Animal Songs

Include the animal name, its sound, and one characteristic.

```
The cow says moo, the cow says moo,
Out in the meadow, the cow says moo.
The cow is big and soft and brown,
The happiest cow in the whole wide town.
```

### Morning Routine

Sequence actions step by step.

```
First I wake up and stretch so high,
Then I brush my teeth — my, oh my!
Wash my face and comb my hair,
Put on my clothes with care.
```

### Bedtime Lullaby

Slow rhythm, gentle imagery, soft rhymes.

```
The stars are twinkling one by one,
The day is done, the day is done.
Close your eyes and rest your head,
Time for sleepy dreams in bed.
```

### Good Manners

Teach social scripts through song.

```
When someone gives a gift to me,
I smile and say "thank you" pleasantly.
When I bump into a friend,
A quick "I'm sorry" makes it mend.
And when I ask for something new,
A friendly "please" will see me through.
```

### Emotion Songs

Name the emotion, validate it, offer comfort.

```
Sometimes I feel so very sad,
And that is okay — it's not so bad.
I take a breath and count to three,
And then the happy comes back to me.
```

## Complete Example Songs

### Example 1: Alphabet Song (Adapted ABCs)

**Title:** A-B-C Let's Learn With Me
**Structure:** AABB, 90 seconds
**Tempo:** 120 BPM

```
[Intro]
La la la, la la la
La la la, let's sing!

[Verse 1]
A is for apple, red and round
B is for ball that bounces on the ground
C is for cat with a soft little purr
D is for dog with a fluffy warm fur

[Chorus]
A-B-C, sing with me
D-E-F-G, happily
H-I-J-K-L-M-N-O-P
Learning letters is fun, you'll see!

[Verse 2]
Q is for queen with a crown so bright
R is for rainbow with colors of light
S is for sun shining up in the sky
T is for turtle who walks slowly by

[Chorus]
A-B-C, sing with me
D-E-F-G, happily
H-I-J-K-L-M-N-O-P
Learning letters is fun, you'll see!

[Bridge]
U-V-W, X-Y-Z
Now I know my ABCs
All the letters, one to twenty-six
Learning them with playful tricks

[Final Chorus]
A-B-C, sing with me
D-E-F-G, happily
H-I-J-K-L-M-N-O-P
Now we know our ABCs — yippee!

[Outro]
A-B-C, you and me!
```

### Example 2: Counting to 10 (With Animals)

**Title:** Ten Little Friends
**Structure:** Cumulative, 90 seconds
**Tempo:** 110 BPM

```
[Verse 1]
One little cat, sitting on a chair
Washing its whiskers with gentle care
One little cat, that's all I see
One little cat, just counting me

[Chorus]
Count with me, one, two, three
Counting animals is fun, you'll see
Count with me, four, five, six
Learning numbers with playful tricks

[Verse 2]
Two little dogs, running in the park
Barking and playing from dawn till dark
Now I see three — three little fish
Swimming around with a gentle swish

[Chorus]
Count with me, one, two, three
Counting animals is fun, you'll see
Count with me, four, five, six
Learning numbers with playful tricks

[Verse 3]
Four little birds, singing in a tree
Five little frogs — ribbit, ribbit, whee!
Six little ducks, waddling in a row
Seven little bunnies, hopping to and fro

[Bridge]
Eight little turtles, walking nice and slow
Nine little butterflies, watch them glow
Ten little friends, all in a line
Counting to ten is super fine!

[Final Chorus]
We counted one, two, three
With animals, so happily!
We counted four, five, six, seven, eight
Numbers are really, really great!
Nine and ten — we did it, friend!
Counting songs will never end!
```

### Example 3: Morning Routine

**Title:** Wake Up, Get Ready!
**Structure:** Verse-Chorus, 2 minutes
**Tempo:** 115 BPM

```
[Verse 1]
Open my eyes, the sun is here
A brand new day is finally here
Stretch my arms up to the sky
Time to get ready, you and I

[Chorus]
Wake up, wash up, brush up, too
So many morning things to do
Wake up, dress up, eat up, play
Ready for a happy day!

[Verse 2]
First I brush my teeth — shine, shine, shine
Up and down, in a straight line
Then I wash my face so clean
The cleanest face you've ever seen

[Chorus]
Wake up, wash up, brush up, too
So many morning things to do
Wake up, dress up, eat up, play
Ready for a happy day!

[Verse 3]
Put on my shirt, my pants, my socks
Zip up my jacket, turn the locks
Time for breakfast, yum, yum, yum
Eat my cereal — here it comes!

[Chorus]
Wake up, wash up, brush up, too
So many morning things to do
Wake up, dress up, eat up, play
Ready for a happy day!

[Bridge]
Shoes on my feet, backpack on my back
Out the door on the morning track
Wave goodbye and off we go
Morning time I love you so

[Final Chorus]
Wake up, wash up, brush up, too
All my morning things are through
Wake up, dress up, eat up, play
I'm ready for a brand new day!
```

### Example 4: Good Manners

**Title:** Magic Words
**Structure:** ABAB, 90 seconds
**Tempo:** 100 BPM

```
[Verse 1]
Please is a word that opens doors
Please is a word that friends adore
When I ask for something new
A friendly please will see me through

[Chorus]
Please and thank you, sorry too
These are magic words for you
They make the people smile and say
"You're welcome!" at the end of day

[Verse 2]
Thank you is a word so kind
Leaving happy thoughts behind
When someone gives a gift to me
I say thank you happily

[Chorus]
Please and thank you, sorry too
These are magic words for you
They make the people smile and say
"You're welcome!" at the end of day

[Verse 3]
Sorry is the word I use
When I've got an oops to choose
If I bump or if I fall
Sorry fixes it for all

[Bridge]
Excuse me when I need to pass
You're welcome — friendly, warm, and class
Share my toys and take a turn
Good manners are the best to learn

[Final Chorus]
Please and thank you, sorry too
All our magic words came through
They make the people smile and say
"What a wonderful, kind day!"
```

### Example 5: Bedtime Lullaby

**Title:** Stars Are Winking
**Structure:** ABCB, 2 minutes
**Tempo:** 80 BPM

```
[Verse 1]
The stars are winking one by one
The day is almost done
The moon is glowing soft and white
The sun has gone to night

[Chorus]
Close your eyes and drift away
To where the dream boats play
The world is quiet, soft, and deep
It's time to go to sleep

[Verse 2]
Your teddy bear is by your side
The gentle night is wide
Your blanket keeps you warm and snug
Like a cozy, loving hug

[Chorus]
Close your eyes and drift away
To where the dream boats play
The world is quiet, soft, and deep
It's time to go to sleep

[Bridge]
Tomorrow is a brand new day
With sunshine, song, and play
But for tonight, just rest your head
And dream beneath your bed

[Final Chorus]
Close your eyes and drift away
To where the dream boats play
I love you more than stars above
Sweet dreams, my little love

[Outro]
Sweet dreams...
Sweet dreams...
Good night.
```
