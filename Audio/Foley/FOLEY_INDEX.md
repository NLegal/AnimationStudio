## Introduction

Foley sounds bring the physical world to life. Unlike sound effects (which are often synthesized or library-sourced), Foley is performed and recorded to match on-screen action precisely. These natural interaction sounds make the world feel tangible — the rustle of clothing, the weight of a book, the texture of paper.

Every Foley sound should:
- Match the character's size and energy
- Feel natural, not mechanical
- Support the story without drawing attention
- Sound warm and familiar to a young audience

**Naming Convention:** `FOLEY_Type_###`

**Example:** `FOLEY_Book_001.wav`

## Foley Production Guidelines

### Recording Environment
- Record in a quiet, treated room (no reverb)
- Use a large-diaphragm condenser mic at 6–12 inches
- Monitor levels to avoid clipping on sudden impacts
- Record multiple takes with varying intensity

### Performance Tips for Foley Artists
- Match the character's size: smaller characters = lighter sounds
- Match the emotional state: happy = bouncier, sad = heavier
- Sync precisely to on-screen action
- Clothing rustle should match the fabric in the scene

### Technical Standards
- Sample rate: 48 kHz
- Bit depth: 24-bit
- Format: .wav mono (center-panned) or stereo if spatial
- Edit out breaths, handling noise, and room tone
- Leave 0.5s of room tone at end for seamless looping

### Volume Relative to Dialogue
| Sound Type | Volume Level |
|------------|-------------|
| Objects (pickup, put down) | -8dB to -12dB |
| Actions (brushing, washing) | -6dB to -10dB |
| Paper/light objects | -12dB to -15dB |
| Furniture | -6dB to -10dB |
| Eating/drinking | -10dB to -14dB |

### File Organization

## Foley Spotting Checklist

Before recording Foley for a scene, review the script and identify:

- [ ] Every hand-object interaction
- [ ] Every body-furniture contact
- [ ] Every fabric movement (clothing, curtains, etc.)
- [ ] Every surface texture change (walking from rug to tile)
- [ ] Every eating, drinking, or hygiene action
- [ ] Every craft or tool use
- [ ] Every book, paper, or writing interaction

---

*Foley breathes life into the silent world. Record with care, edit with precision, and always prioritize natural warmth over technical perfection.*
