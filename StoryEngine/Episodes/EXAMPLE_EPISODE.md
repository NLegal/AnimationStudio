## Story Structure

### Opening (0:00-0:35)

*Emotion: Curiosity, Warmth*

The camera pans across Sunny Meadows Playground on a beautiful spring morning. Birds chirp. Butterflies flutter. Lily Bunny hops onto the scene carrying a bright red ball.

**Narrator:** "It is a sunny morning in Sunny Meadows. Lily Bunny is at the playground with her favorite toy — a bright red ball!"

**Lily Bunny:** "Hello, friends! Look at my red ball. It goes bounce, bounce, bounce!" *(bounces the ball)*

**Interactive Moment:** "Can you say hello to Lily? Wave your hand! Hello!"

---

### Goal (0:35-1:00)

*Emotion: Excitement*

Ben Bear arrives at the playground. He sees Lily playing with the red ball.

**Ben Bear:** "Hi Lily! Your ball looks so fun. Can I play too?"

**Lily Bunny:** *(hugs the ball tightly)* "This is MY ball. I want to play by myself."

**Narrator:** "Lily loves her red ball. She wants to play all by herself. But Ben looks sad."

**Interactive Moment:** "Oh no! How do you think Ben feels? Can you make a sad face?"

---

### Problem (1:00-1:30)

*Emotion: Concern, Thinking*

Lily tries to play alone. She bounces the ball, but it doesn't feel as fun by herself. She looks at Ben playing with the blue ball alone too.

**Narrator:** "Lily bounces the ball up and down. But playing alone is not so fun."

**Lily Bunny:** *(looking around)* "Hmm... bouncing by myself is a little boring."

**Ben Bear:** *(sitting alone with blue ball)* "I wish someone would play with me."

**Narrator:** "Lily has a problem. She wants to play, but she doesn't want to share her ball."

**Interactive Moment:** "What should Lily do? Should she share? Point to your answer!"

---

### Discovery (1:30-2:00)

*Emotion: Realization, Hope*

Miss Owl arrives at the playground. She sees Lily playing alone and Ben playing alone.

**Miss Owl:** "Good morning, Lily. Good morning, Ben. Why are you playing in different places?"

**Lily Bunny:** "I want to play with my ball, but I don't want to share."

**Miss Owl:** "I see. Lily, do you know what happens when you share?"

**Lily Bunny:** "You... lose your toy?"

**Miss Owl:** *(gently)* "Actually, when you share, you get something even better."

**Lily Bunny:** "What is it?"

**Miss Owl:** "A friend to play with! When you share, you both have fun together."

---

### Learning (2:00-2:45)

*Emotion: Focus, Engagement*

Miss Owl teaches Lily about sharing.

**Miss Owl:** "When you share, you take turns. First you play. Then Ben plays. Then you play again."

**Lily Bunny:** "Both of us get a turn?"

**Miss Owl:** "Yes! And you both get to have fun. Let's practice. Lily, can you say 'want to play together'?"

**Lily Bunny:** "Want to play together?"

**Miss Owl:** "Perfect! Now Ben, what do you say when someone shares with you?"

**Ben Bear:** "Thank you!"

**Miss Owl:** "That is right! Sharing and saying thank you."

**Interactive Moment:** "Let's practice! Say it with me: 'Want to play together?' Great! Now say 'Thank you!' Wonderful!"

---

### Practice (2:45-3:30)

*Emotion: Determination, Confidence*

Lily decides to try sharing.

**Lily Bunny:** *(walks to Ben)* "Ben... do you want to play together?"

**Ben Bear:** *(smiles big)* "Yes! Thank you, Lily!"

Lily gives Ben the red ball. Ben bounces it.

**Ben Bear:** "This is so fun! Bounce, bounce, bounce!"

**Lily Bunny:** *(clapping)* "Yay! Ben is having fun with my ball!"

**Ben Bear:** "Here is your turn, Lily. And you can play with my blue ball too!"

**Lily Bunny:** "Two balls! We can bounce them together!"

They bounce the red ball and blue ball together, taking turns.

**Narrator:** "Lily shared her red ball. Ben shared his blue ball. Now they are both playing together!"

**Interactive Moment:** "Can you bounce like Lily and Ben? Bounce, bounce, bounce!"

---

### Success (3:30-3:50)

*Emotion: Pride, Joy*

Lily and Ben play happily together.

**Lily Bunny:** "Sharing is fun! We both get to play!"

**Ben Bear:** "Playing together is better than playing alone."

**Miss Owl:** "You did a wonderful job sharing, Lily. How do you feel?"

**Lily Bunny:** "I feel happy! Happy that Ben is my friend!"

**Interactive Moment:** "Can you clap for Lily and Ben? Clap, clap, clap!"

---

### Celebration (3:50-4:20)

*Emotion: Joy, Elation*

**Song Placement:** Ending Song (Celebration Song) — 45 seconds

**Song Title:** "Sharing is Caring"
**Style:** Upbeat, cheerful
**Interactive:** Clap-along rhythm

*Lyrics:*
*"Sharing, sharing, it's so fun / Share with everyone!*
*First your turn, then my turn / Learning is so fun!*
*Bounce the ball and pass it on / Play until the day is done / 
Sharing, sharing, makes us smile / Let's share all the while!"*

---

### Goodbye (4:20-4:30)

*Emotion: Contentment, Warmth*

**Lily Bunny:** "Today we learned about sharing!"

**Ben Bear:** "When you share, you make a friend happy!"

**Lily Bunny:** "Try sharing with your friends today!"

**Ben Bear:** "Bye bye, friends! See you next time!"

**Narrator:** "And that is how Lily Bunny learned to share. Sharing makes everyone happy!"

**Interactive Moment:** "Wave goodbye to Lily and Ben! See you next time!"

---

```
    ↓

    ↓

    ↓

    ↓
Joy (Celebration — Song and dance)
    ↓
Warmth (Goodbye — Content and fulfilled)
```
