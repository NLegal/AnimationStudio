# Series & Season Planner Guide

## Purpose

The Series and Season Planner organizes episodes into coherent seasons, plans holiday content in advance, ensures long-term curriculum progression, and maintains variety across the entire series. It transforms the Story Engine from a single-episode generator into a multi-season content production system.

---

### Season Structure

Each season contains 26 episodes (half a year at one-per-week release):

```
Season Template:
  - 20 regular episodes (standard curriculum rotation)
  - 4 holiday / seasonal specials
  - 2 review / compilation episodes

Episode spacing:
  - 1 new episode released per week
  - Holiday episodes align with calendar dates
  - Mid-season review at episode 13
  - Season finale is a special event
```

### Holiday Episode Rules

| Rule | Explanation |
|---|---|
| Holiday episodes are evergreen | Avoid mentioning specific years or dates |
| Cultural inclusivity | Include non-religious framing where possible |
| Seasonal atmosphere | Weather and decorations match the holiday |
| Learning objective first | Holiday is theme, not the lesson |
| Holiday vocabulary | Introduce holiday-related words naturally |
| Celebration focus | Emphasize family, giving, and togetherness |

## Episode Progression

### Within a Season

```
Early episodes (1-8):
  - Focus on single, simple concepts
  - Shorter duration (2-3 minutes)
  - Younger vocabulary level (ages 2-3)
  - More repetition, slower pace

Middle episodes (9-18):
  - Build on concepts from early episodes
  - Medium duration (3-4 minutes)
  - Mid-range vocabulary (ages 3-4)
  - Moderate repetition, faster pace

Late episodes (19-26):
  - Combine concepts from earlier episodes
  - Longer duration (4-5 minutes)
  - Extended vocabulary (ages 4-5)
  - Less repetition, natural conversations

Season finale (26):
  - Special episode featuring all characters
  - Celebration of the season's learning
  - Preview of next season's theme
```

### Across Seasons

Season-to-season progression ensures children grow with the content:

```
Age 2-3: Season 1 (Meet Characters)
  - Basic vocabulary, simple stories
  - High repetition, slow pace
  - Short attention span episodes (2 min)

Age 3-4: Seasons 2-3 (Colors + Numbers)
  - Building vocabulary, longer stories
  - Moderate repetition
  - Medium episodes (3 min)

Age 4-5: Seasons 4-5 (Animals + School)
  - Complex sentences, multi-scene stories
  - Natural repetition
  - Medium episodes (4 min)

Age 5-6: Seasons 6-8 (Science + Music + My World)
  - Longer conversations, abstract concepts
  - Reinforced through application
  - Longer episodes (5 min)
```

### Episode Pacing

```
Weekly cadence:
  Monday    → New episode releases
  Wednesday → Repeat previous episode (reinforcement)
  Friday    → Interactive activity related to the episode

Monthly cadence:
  Week 1-3 → Educational episodes (new concepts)
  Week 4   → Review episode + song compilation
```

## Planning Rules

| Rule | Description |
|---|---|
| Plan 6 months ahead | Always have the next season fully planned |
| Reserve holidays first | Block holiday episodes before filling regular slots |
| Balance curriculum | Ensure even distribution across learning areas |
| Rotate main characters | No single character dominates a season |
| Vary locations | Introduce new locations each season |
| Build toward finale | Season finale should feel special and cumulative |
| Preview next season | Final episode hints at the next season's theme |
| Leave flexibility | Reserve 2-3 open slots for timely content |
