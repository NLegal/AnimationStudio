# Objective

After completing:

* **Phase 1** → Characters
* **Phase 2** → World

The next step is creating **everything the characters interact with.**

Think of this as building your own Pixar prop warehouse.

Every toy.

Every chair.

Every apple.

Every toothbrush.

Every musical instrument.

Every book.

Every tree.

Every classroom object.

Every holiday decoration.

Once built, these assets become reusable forever.

---

# Philosophy

Never create the same object twice.

If a toy train appears in Episode 3, it should be the exact same toy train in Episode 500.

This creates:

* Brand consistency
* Faster production
* Better AI consistency
* Lower rendering costs
* Easier automation

# Long-Term Vision

By the completion of Phase 3, your studio will possess the equivalent of a professional animation prop department. Future productions will no longer create objects from scratch—they will assemble scenes from a growing library of approved, consistent assets.

Combined with Phase 1 (Characters) and Phase 2 (World), this asset library becomes the foundation for rapid, scalable content creation. New episodes will primarily involve selecting existing characters, environments, and props, then generating only the animations and story-specific interactions, dramatically reducing production time while maintaining a recognizable visual identity.
