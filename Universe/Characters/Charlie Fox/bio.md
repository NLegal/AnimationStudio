# Charlie Fox

## Basic Information
- **Name:** Charlie Fox
- **Nickname:** Charlie
- **Species:** Fox (orange fur, white chest)
- **Gender:** Male
- **Age:** 5
- **Birthday:** Unknown (loves every day like an adventure)
- **Height:** Medium
- **Weight:** 15-18 kg
- **Favorite Color:** Green
- **Favorite Food:** Berries, forest snacks
- **Favorite Song:** Adventure tunes he hums on trails
- **Favorite Animal:** Foxes (but he also loves owls)
- **Favorite Toy:** Compass, binoculars
- **Favorite Game:** Hide and seek, treasure hunts
- **Favorite Activity:** Exploring, climbing, camping
- **Favorite Season:** Autumn (crunchy leaves!)
- **Favorite Holiday:** Any day he can explore

- **Eye Color:** Bright green
- **Fur Color:** Orange with white chest and belly
- **Skin Color:** Black (nose), pink (inner ears)
- **Ear Shape:** Pointed fox ears, always perked
- **Tail:** Large fluffy orange with white tip
- **Body Shape:** Slim, athletic, agile proportions
- **Face Shape:** Narrow, pointed snout
- **Nose:** Small round black
- **Mouth:** Wide grin, mischievous
- **Eyebrows:** Expressive, often raised
- **Eyelashes:** Short, visible
- **Teeth:** Small pointed canines (when grinning)
- **Freckles:** Scattered across nose and cheeks
- **Dimples:** When he grins wide
- **Accessories:** Compass on a string, small backpack
- **Shoes:** Brown hiking boots
- **Hat:** None usually
- **Glasses:** Not needed
- **Backpack:** Green explorer backpack

## Personality
- **Primary:** Adventurous, Curious, Brave
- **Secondary:** Funny, Creative, Energetic
- **Tertiary:** Acts before thinking, Sometimes reckless

## Skills
- Finding hidden things (sharp eyes)
- Nature knowledge (knows every plant and trail)
- Reading maps (natural navigator)
- Camping (sets up tents in minutes)
- Climbing (scales trees and rocks easily)

## Weaknesses
