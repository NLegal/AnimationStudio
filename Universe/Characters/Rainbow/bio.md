# Rainbow

## Basic Information
- **Name:** Rainbow
- **Nickname:** Bow
- **Species:** Rainbow
- **Gender:** Neutral (she/her for warmth)
- **Age:** Ageless
- **Favorite Color:** All (every color matters)
- **Favorite Food:** Sunshine droplets (sun + rain mix)
- **Favorite Activity:** Brightening skies, appearing after rain
- **Favorite Game:** Hide-and-seek (appearing unexpectedly)
- **Favorite Season:** Spring (perfect rain-sun balance)
- **Favorite Weather:** Rain then sun

## Appearance
- **Colors:** Red (`#F0A0C0`), orange (`#F5A623`), yellow (`#F5D442`), green (`#7EC850`), blue (`#4A90D9`), indigo, violet (`#C8A0D0`)
- **Shape:** Perfect arch across the sky
- **Texture:** Shimmering, translucent glow
- **Edges:** Soft, fading into sky
- **Size:** Varies — usually spans horizon to horizon
- **Face:** Gentle, happy expression visible within the glow
- **Presence:** Warm, celebratory feeling
- **Movement:** Stationary but shimmering — edges pulse gently

## Forms / States
| Variant | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Full Arc | Classic rainbow from ground to ground |
| Double | Fainter second arch above, colors reversed |
| Partial | Small arc just appearing after quick rain |
| Sunset Rainbow | Deep, warm-toned, golden glow |
| Morning Rainbow | Soft pastel, dew-fresh |
| Fading | Slowly dissolving, colors one by one |
| Bright | Clear sky after rain, colors vivid |
| Baby Rainbow | Small, cute, low to ground |

## Personality
- **Primary:** Magical guide, Celebratory, Hopeful
- **Secondary:** Cheerful, Bright, Warm
- **Tertiary:** Quiet wisdom, Unity-focused, Patient

## Skills
- Appearing after rain (like magic)
- Brightening skies (makes everything better)
- Color magic (brings colors together)
- Unity lessons (each color needs the others)
- Hope symbol (makes everyone smile)
- Bridging earth and sky

## Weaknesses
- Can't appear without both rain and sun
- Fades slowly and disappears
- Can't be touched (just light)
- Only appears at certain angles
- Vulnerable to dark clouds

## Catchphrases
1. "Every color shines brighter together."
2. "After the rain comes the rainbow."
3. "Follow me to wonder!"
4. "You are my favorite color."
5. "Look up — I'm here!"
