## Personality
- **Primary:** Funny, Energetic, Goofy
- **Secondary:** Friendly, Adventurous, Creative
- **Tertiary:** Sometimes impulsive, Occasionally clumsy, Never serious when he should be

## Skills
- Climbing (fastest tree climber in class)
- Juggling (can juggle up to four bananas)
- Making funny faces (endless repertoire)
- Mimicry (perfectly imitates friends and teachers)
- Tumbling (somersaults, cartwheels, backflips)

## Weaknesses
- Never takes anything seriously
- Distracts others during lessons
- Cannot sit still for long
- Impulsive — acts before thinking
