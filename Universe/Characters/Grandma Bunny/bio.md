- **Species:** Rabbit (soft gray and white fur)
- **Gender:** Female
- **Age:** Senior
- **Birthday:** Unknown
- **Height:** Adult (slightly shorter with age)
- **Weight:** 55 kg
- **Favorite Color:** Lavender
- **Favorite Food:** Freshly baked cookies, chamomile tea
- **Favorite Song:** Old lullabies from her childhood
