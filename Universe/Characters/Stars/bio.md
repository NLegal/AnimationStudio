# Stars

## Basic Information
- **Name:** Stars
- **Nickname:** Twinkle, Sparkle (group names)
- **Species:** Stars (group character)
- **Gender:** Neutral (they/them collectively)
- **Age:** Ageless
- **Favorite Color:** All (gold, blue, pink, white)
- **Favorite Food:** Moon dust
- **Favorite Activity:** Twinkling in patterns, guiding at night
- **Favorite Game:** Making constellations, follow-the-leader twinkle
- **Favorite Season:** Winter (nights are longest)
- **Favorite Weather:** Clear skies

## Appearance
- **Colors:** Gold (`#F5D442`), blue (`#4A90D9`), pink (`#F0A0C0`), white
- **Shapes:** Classic five-point stars — varying sizes
- **Glow:** Soft twinkling light, each star slightly different
- **Texture:** Sparkly, luminous points
- **Sizes:** Mix of big bright and tiny delicate
- **Grouping:** Cluster together, form patterns
- **Movement:** Gentle twinkle, slow drift across sky
- **Trails:** Soft shimmer trails when they move
- **Faces:** Tiny smiling faces in their centers (cute)

## Forms / States
| Variant | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Twinkle Pattern | Alternating bright-dim in sequence |
| Constellation | Temporary shape — heart, crown, animal |
| Shooting Star | Quick dash across sky, trailing sparkle |
| Morning Fade | Dimming slowly as Sun rises |
| Night Bright | Brightest, clearest, dancing together |
| Playful | Zipping around, hiding behind Moon |
| Wishing Moment | Still, bright, listening to wishes |
| Lost Star | Small, drifting from group, scared |

## Personality
- **Primary:** Cheerful, Sparkly, Playful as a group
- **Secondary:** Gentle, Guiding, Hopeful
- **Tertiary:** Shy individually, Brave together, Loyal

## Skills
- Twinkling in patterns (message blinking)
- Guiding at night (navigation help)
- Making wishes come true (pretend magic)
- Scene transitions (day to night helpers)
- Forming constellations
- Singing silently (light vibrations)

## Weaknesses
- Can't be seen during day
- Individual stars get lost from group
- Too small to help alone
- Fade in city lights
- Vulnerable to cloudy nights

## Catchphrases
1. "Twinkle together!"
2. "Make a wish!"
3. "Follow our light."
4. "We're all connected — like constellations."
5. "Every star matters."
