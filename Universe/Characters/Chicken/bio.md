## Personality
- **Primary:** Responsible, Organized, Helpful
- **Secondary:** Early riser, Energetic, Cheerful
- **Tertiary:** Bossy, Goes to bed too early, Perfectionist

## Skills
- Waking everyone up (never sleeps in)
- Organizing (everything has its place)
- Time management (always on schedule)
- Egg collecting (gentle and careful)
- Cleaning (keeps things tidy)
- Planning (makes lists for everything)

## Weaknesses
- Goes to bed very early — misses evening fun
- Bossy — tells everyone what to do
- Too rigid — doesn't like schedule changes
- Cannot sleep past sunrise
