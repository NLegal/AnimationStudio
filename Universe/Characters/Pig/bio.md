## Personality
- **Primary:** Cheerful, Food-loving, Friendly
- **Secondary:** Generous, Creative, Warm-hearted

- Cooking (makes delicious meals for friends)
- Baking (cakes, cookies, pies)
- Tasting (expert flavor tester)
