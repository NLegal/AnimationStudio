# Grandpa Bunny

## Basic Information
- **Name:** Grandpa Bunny
- **Nickname:** Grandpa, Pop-Pop
- **Species:** Rabbit (salt-and-pepper brown fur)
- **Gender:** Male
- **Age:** Senior
- **Birthday:** Unknown
- **Height:** Adult (stooped with age)
- **Weight:** 60 kg
- **Favorite Color:** Brown
- **Favorite Food:** Fresh vegetables from his garden, warm soup
- **Favorite Song:** Old folk songs, humming while gardening
- **Favorite Animal:** Bunnies, birds
- **Favorite Toy:** Walking stick (whittled himself)
- **Favorite Game:** Chess, checkers
- **Favorite Activity:** Gardening, storytelling, woodworking
- **Favorite Season:** Spring (planting season)
- **Favorite Holiday:** Harvest festival

## Appearance
- **Eye Color:** Warm, twinkling brown
- **Fur Color:** Salt-and-pepper brown (graying with age)
- **Skin Color:** Light pink (nose, inner ears, paw pads)
- **Ear Shape:** Long, droopy with age
- **Tail:** Soft round cotton ball
- **Body Shape:** Lean, slightly stooped
- **Face Shape:** Wrinkled, kind, weathered
- **Nose:** Medium round pink
- **Mouth:** Gentle, knowing smile
- **Eyebrows:** Bushy, gray
- **Eyelashes:** Short, light
- **Teeth:** Small buck teeth (one slightly crooked)
- **Freckles:** Age spots on cheeks
- **Dimples:** Deep wrinkles when smiling
- **Accessories:** Brown suspenders, walking stick
- **Shoes:** Brown worn-in boots
- **Hat:** Brown newsboy cap
- **Glasses:** Half-moon reading glasses
- **Backpack:** None (prefers a woven basket)

## Personality
- **Primary:** Patient, Wise, Gentle
- **Secondary:** Knowledgeable, Calm, Humorous
- **Tertiary:** Slow-moving, Set in his ways, Tells long stories

## Skills
- Gardening (grows the best vegetables in town)
- Woodworking (carves beautiful toys)
- Storytelling (endless tales from childhood)
- Whittling (makes walking sticks and figurines)
- Patience (never rushes anything)

## Weaknesses
- Moves slowly
- Tells very long stories
- Set in his ways
- Slightly hard of hearing
