# Alien

## Basic Information
- **Name:** Alien
- **Nickname:** Zee
- **Species:** Alien (unknown origin)
- **Gender:** Neutral (they/them)
- **Age:** Unknown
- **Favorite Color:** All colors
- **Favorite Food:** Space snacks (glowing)
- **Favorite Activity:** Discovering Earth things, floating
- **Favorite Game:** Playing with Earth objects, star counting
- **Favorite Season:** All (everything is new!)
- **Favorite Weather:** All (each is fascinating)
- **Weight:** 15 kg

## Appearance
- **Skin Color:** Mint green (`#A8E6CF`)
- **Eyes:** Three big eyes — left pink (`#F0A0C0`), center yellow (`#F5D442`), right blue (`#4A90D9`)
- **Antenna:** Small on top of head, with glowing orb tips
- **Body Shape:** Slim, small frame
- **Arms:** Spindly, long, three-fingered hands
- **Legs:** Thin, float rather than walk
- **Movement:** Floating/hovering (drifts gently)
- **Mouth:** Small, expressive
- **Skin Texture:** Smooth, slightly luminescent
- **Accessories:** None natural; sometimes wears human things (backwards)

## Forms / States
| Variant | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Floating | Drifting gently, eyes wide, curious |

## Personality
- **Primary:** Curious about Earth, Friendly, Playful
- **Secondary:** Confused by human things, Enthusiastic, Adaptable
- **Tertiary:** Eager to learn, Easily amazed, Slightly clumsy

## Skills
- Floating (permanent levitation)
- Translating (speaks all languages through bleeps)
- Discovering Earth things with wonder
- Star knowledge (knows every constellation)
- Fitting in small spaces
- Glowing (soft luminescence)

## Weaknesses
- Gets lost easily (no sense of direction on Earth)
- Doesn't understand Earth customs
- Translation sometimes wrong (hilarious misunderstandings)
- Can't touch water (sizzles slightly)
- Homesick for space
