# Lily Bunny

## Basic Information
- **Name:** Lily Bunny
- **Nickname:** Lily
- **Species:** Bunny (soft white fur, pink inner ears)
- **Gender:** Female
- **Age:** Preschool (4-5 years)
- **Birthday:** March 15
- **Height:** 75cm (adorably small)
- **Favorite Color:** Pink
- **Favorite Food:** Carrot cupcakes, strawberry smoothies
- **Favorite Song:** "The Happy Bunnies Song" (her own made-up tune)
- **Favorite Animal:** Bunnies (but she also loves butterflies)
- **Favorite Toy:** Fluffy the Bunny (her stuffed animal)
- **Favorite Game:** Hide and seek, hopscotch
- **Favorite Activity:** Drawing, singing, helping friends
- **Favorite Season:** Spring (flowers blooming!)
- **Favorite Holiday:** Easter (obviously)

## Appearance
- **Eye Color:** Big bright green
- **Fur Color:** Soft white with light pink inner ears
- **Skin Color:** Light pink (nose, inner ears, paw pads)
- **Ear Shape:** Long, floppy (tied with a blue bow)
- **Tail:** Small round cotton ball
- **Body Shape:** Soft, rounded proportions — toddler-like
- **Face Shape:** Round, chubby cheeks
- **Nose:** Small round pink
- **Mouth:** Simple, friendly, large smile
- **Eyebrows:** Expressive, soft arches
- **Eyelashes:** Long, visible
- **Teeth:** Cute buck teeth (when smiling wide)
- **Freckles:** Light scattering across nose
- **Dimples:** Visible when smiling
- **Accessories:** Blue bow on left ear (signature), small backpack
- **Shoes:** Pink Mary Janes
- **Hat:** Sun hat (summer)
- **Glasses:** Not needed
- **Backpack:** Pink bunny-shaped backpack

## Personality
- **Primary:** Happy, Curious, Kind
- **Secondary:** Brave, Helpful, Creative
- **Tertiary:** Sometimes shy, Occasionally clumsy, Energetic in the morning

## Skills
- Drawing (loves to draw her friends)
- Singing (makes up her own songs)
- Counting (learning to count to 100)
- Dancing (hop-hop dance)
- Gardening (helps in the school garden)
- Making friends (befriends everyone)

## Weaknesses
- Afraid of thunder
- Gets distracted by butterflies
- Too trusting of strangers
- Sometimes forgets to share

## Catchphrases

2. "Hi, I'm Lily! What's your name?"
3. "Hoppity-hop, let's go!"
4. "That's wonderful!"
5. "I have an idea!"
