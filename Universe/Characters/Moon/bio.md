# Moon

## Basic Information
- **Name:** Moon
- **Nickname:** Luna
- **Species:** Moon
- **Gender:** Female (she/her)
- **Age:** Ageless
- **Favorite Color:** Silver-white
- **Favorite Food:** Stardust
- **Favorite Activity:** Night watching, bedtime stories
- **Favorite Game:** Peekaboo (with clouds)
- **Favorite Season:** Autumn (crisp clear nights)
- **Favorite Weather:** Clear sky (no clouds)

## Appearance
- **Color:** Soft silver-white glow
- **Phases:** Crescent, half, gibbous, full circle
- **Face:** Kind smiling face — craters form gentle features
- **Glow:** Soft, warm white light
- **Texture:** Smooth, luminous surface
- **Eyes:** Gentle, sleepy, crescent-shaped when smiling
- **Mouth:** Soft warm curve
- **Cheeks:** Rosy blush visible on full moon
- **Companions:** Surrounded by tiny star friends
- **Size:** Large in the night sky

## Forms / States
| Variant | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Full Moon | Complete circle, brightest, face clear |
| Crescent | Thin smile in sky, smallest glow |
| Gibbous | Mostly full, nearly there |
| Half Moon | Split evenly — gentle profile |
| Shy Moon | Partially behind cloud, peeking |
| Bedtime Moon | Yawning, softest glow, sleepy eyes |
| Guardian Moon | Full, bright, watchful over sleeping world |
| New Moon | Invisible — resting, no glow |

## Personality
- **Primary:** Calm, Nighttime guardian, Soothing
- **Secondary:** Sleepy, Gentle, Wise
- **Tertiary:** Shy, Nurturing, Patient

## Skills
- Night light (gentle glow for the world)
- Bedtime stories (silent stories in moonlight)
- Lullabies (wind hums through her light)
- Calming fears (soft presence chases monsters)
- Guiding travelers (lights the path)
- Phasing (growing and shrinking gracefully)

## Weaknesses
- Disappears during the day
- Gets shy when clouds pass
- New moon = invisible (no light)
- Can't warm anything (reflected light only)
- Lonely during eclipses

## Catchphrases
1. "Sweet dreams begin now."
2. "I'll watch over you."
3. "Goodnight, little one."
4. "The stars are singing — listen."
5. "Sleep is just another adventure."

| Situation | Reaction |
|-----------|----------|
| Children sleeping | Peaceful — full soft glow, warm smile |

| Full moon night | Proud — brightest, roundest smile |
