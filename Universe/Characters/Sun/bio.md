# Sun

## Basic Information
- **Name:** Sun
- **Nickname:** Sunny
- **Species:** Sun
- **Gender:** Male (he/him)
- **Age:** Ageless
- **Favorite Color:** Golden yellow (`#F5D442`)
- **Favorite Food:** Morning dew (sipped through rays)
- **Favorite Activity:** Waking everyone up, growing plants
- **Favorite Game:** Peekaboo (behind clouds)
- **Favorite Season:** Summer (longest time in sky)
- **Favorite Weather:** Clear blue sky

## Appearance
- **Color:** Bright golden yellow (`#F5D442`)
- **Shape:** Perfect circle
- **Rays:** Warm golden rays radiating in all directions
- **Face:** Big cheerful smile, rosy cheeks
- **Eyes:** Warm, bright, crescent-shape when smiling
- **Mouth:** Wide happy smile
- **Cheeks:** Rosy pink (`#F0A0C0` warmth)
- **Glow:** Warm, golden halo around edges
- **Size:** Large and commanding in sky
- **Movement:** Arcs across the sky (daily journey)

## Forms / States
| Variant | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Morning Rise | Peeking over horizon, half-awake rays |
| Noon Bright | High, brightest, rays extended proudly |
| Afternoon Warm | Golden, softer rays, warmest glow |
| Sunset | Deep orange (`#F5A623`), sinking, long rays |
| Peekaboo | Behind cloud, half-hidden, playful smile |
| Sleepy | Setting, yawning, rays drooping |
| Happy | Brightest, rays waving in all directions |
| Gentle | Warm, soft, encouraging — morning growth |

## Personality
- **Primary:** Warm, Energetic, Morning guide
- **Secondary:** Encouraging, Playful, Cheerful
- **Tertiary:** Punctual (always rises on time), Warm-hearted, Protective

## Skills
- Waking everyone up (gentle morning rays)
- Growing plants (photosynthesis magic)
- Warming the day (perfect temperature)
- Peekaboo (hiding behind clouds)
- Daily sky travel (arcing reliably)
- Sunset painting (colors the sky beautiful)

## Weaknesses
- Too hot sometimes (must dial it back)
- Sets at night (can't stay)
- Blinding if stared at (must look away)
- Lonely at night (Moon takes over)
- Can't reach the cold places well
