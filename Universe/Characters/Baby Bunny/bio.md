# Baby Bunny

## Basic Information
- **Name:** Baby Bunny
- **Nickname:** Baby, Little One
- **Species:** Rabbit (soft white fur)
- **Gender:** Male
- **Age:** 2
- **Birthday:** Unknown
- **Height:** Very small
- **Weight:** 10-12 kg
- **Favorite Color:** Doesn't have one yet (loves all bright colors)
- **Favorite Food:** Everything (especially carrots)
- **Favorite Song:** Any song with clapping
- **Favorite Animal:** Bunnies
- **Favorite Toy:** Bubbles, squeaky toys, his tiny backpack
- **Favorite Game:** Peek-a-boo, chase
- **Favorite Activity:** Blowing bubbles, following Lily, exploring
- **Favorite Season:** All of them (everything is new!)
- **Favorite Holiday:** Any holiday with presents

## Appearance
- **Eye Color:** Big bright blue
- **Fur Color:** Soft white (fluffy)
- **Skin Color:** Light pink (nose, inner ears, paw pads)
- **Ear Shape:** Long, slightly floppy, one ear sometimes bent
- **Tail:** Tiny round cotton ball
- **Body Shape:** Very small, round, toddler proportions
- **Face Shape:** Round, chubby cheeks
- **Nose:** Tiny round pink
- **Mouth:** Small, always making expressions
- **Eyebrows:** Light, expressive
- **Eyelashes:** Very long, visible
- **Teeth:** Two tiny bottom teeth showing
- **Freckles:** None
- **Dimples:** Always visible
- **Accessories:** Tiny backpack (favorite item)
- **Shoes:** Tiny soft-soled shoes
- **Hat:** Tiny sun hat (summer)
- **Glasses:** Not needed
- **Backpack:** Tiny white backpack with bunny ears (never leaves home without it)

## Personality
- **Primary:** Curious, Energetic, Happy
- **Secondary:** Affectionate, Determined, Observant
- **Tertiary:** Easily distracted, Limited vocabulary, Clumsy

## Skills
- Blowing bubbles (his proudest achievement)
- Saying new words (learning more every day)
- Giving hugs (the best hugs)
- Finding trouble (amazing at this)
- Following Lily (expert-level shadowing)

## Weaknesses
- Still learning words (limited communication)
- Gets into everything
- Short attention span
- Falls down often
- Can't reach high things

## Catchphrases
1. "Again!"
2. "Mine!"
3. "Wook!" (Look)
4. "No-no-no!" (runs away giggling)
