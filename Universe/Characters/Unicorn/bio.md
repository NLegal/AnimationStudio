# Unicorn

## Basic Information
- **Name:** Unicorn
- **Nickname:** Uni
- **Species:** Unicorn
- **Gender:** Female
- **Age:** Ageless
- **Favorite Color:** Rainbow
- **Favorite Food:** Moonbeam berries
- **Favorite Activity:** Creating rainbows, inspiring art
- **Favorite Game:** Star-gazing, hide-and-seek (bad at it)
- **Favorite Season:** Spring (flowers bloom for her)
- **Favorite Weather:** After rain (rainbow time)
- **Weight:** 150 kg

## Appearance
- **Coat Color:** White (pure, slight shimmer)
- **Mane Color:** Rainbow (red `#F0A0C0` through violet `#C8A0D0`)
- **Tail Color:** Rainbow (matching mane)
- **Horn Color:** Golden (`#F5D442`), spiral, glows softly
- **Eye Color:** Purple (`#C8A0D0`), large, kind
- **Hooves:** Silver, polished
- **Body Shape:** Elegant, graceful, pony-proportioned
- **Face Shape:** Refined, gentle slope
- **Nose:** Soft muzzle, pinkish
- **Mouth:** Gentle smile
- **Eyebrows:** Fine, expressive
- **Eyelashes:** Long, beautiful
- **Legs:** Long, slender, graceful
- **Accessories:** None needed (horn is enough)

## Forms / States
| Variant | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Grazing | Head down, mane flowing, peaceful |
| Galloping | Magical stride, rainbow trail, mane streaming |
| Standing | Proud, elegant, horn catching light |
| Sleeping | Lying down, head on hooves, horn dimmed |
| Nervous | Horn glowing brighter, looking around |
| Artistic | Creating rainbows with horn, eyes closed |
| Playful | Prancing, inviting friends to follow |

## Personality
- **Primary:** Kind, Inspires imagination, Graceful
- **Secondary:** Mysterious, Gentle, Wise
- **Tertiary:** Playful (in private), Sentimental, Protective of dreams

## Skills
- Creating rainbows (horn magic)
- Granting wishes (pretend play)
- Inspiring art and creativity
- Night sky navigation
- Walking on clouds
- Galloping without sound

## Weaknesses
- Horn glows when nervous (gives her away)
- Can't hide well (too sparkly)
- Too magical for ordinary games
- Gets lonely — needs believers
- Takes things too seriously sometimes

## Catchphrases
1. "Believe in wonderful things."
2. "Magic is everywhere — look closer."
3. "You are extraordinary."
4. "Close your eyes and wish."
5. "Rainbows are smiles from the sky."
