# Friendly Dinosaur (Brontosaurus)

## Basic Information
- **Name:** Friendly Dinosaur
- **Nickname:** Dino
- **Species:** Brontosaurus (baby)
- **Gender:** Male
- **Age:** Young
- **Favorite Color:** Green (`#7EC850`)
- **Favorite Food:** Leaves, flowers
- **Favorite Activity:** Reaching high branches, slow walks
- **Favorite Game:** Neck games (gently lowering head)
- **Favorite Season:** Summer (leaves are sweetest)
- **Favorite Weather:** Warm sunshine
- **Weight:** 500 kg

## Appearance
- **Skin Color:** Blue-green (`#A8E6CF` blend)
- **Texture:** Pebbly, gentle pattern
- **Eye Color:** Gentle round brown
- **Body Shape:** Long neck, bulky body, gentle giant
- **Face Shape:** Broad, friendly snout
- **Nose:** Wide nostrils, soft
- **Mouth:** Small friendly smile
- **Eyebrows:** Soft, expressive
- **Eyelashes:** Visible, gentle
- **Teeth:** Flat plant-eater teeth (rarely shown)
- **Neck:** Very long, flexible, used for hugs
- **Tail:** Very long, sometimes bumpy
- **Legs:** Thick, sturdy, four-legged
- **Claws:** Small rounded nails
- **Accessories:** Sometimes a flower crown (gift from friends)

## Forms / States
| Variant | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Eating | Long neck reaching up, gentle munching |
| Walking | Slow, heavy steps, tail swaying |
| Neck Hug | Lowering head to wrap around friend |
| Sitting | Resting on haunches, head low |
| Sleeping | Curled in a circle, tail wrapped around |
| Curious | Neck extended, head tilted, sniffing |
| Playing | Gentle stomping, chasing bubbles |

## Personality
- **Primary:** Gentle giant, Loves plants, Curious
- **Secondary:** Patient, Kind, Protective
- **Tertiary:** Slow to anger, Warm-hearted, Shy about size

## Skills
- Reaching high places (best branch picker)
- Plant knowledge (knows every flower)
- Slow walks (enjoys the journey)
- Neck hugs (wraps neck around friends)
- Gentle stomping (ground feels safe)
- Remembering paths (great explorer)

## Weaknesses
- Tail bumps into things (doesn't know its length)
- Too big for some games
- Slow — can't do fast activities
- Afraid of loud noises
- Lonely when head can't reach friends' height

## Catchphrases
1. "Big hearts can be gentle."
2. "I'll reach that for you!"
3. "Slow and steady wins the snack."
4. "Neck hug?"
5. "The tallest trees have the sweetest leaves."
