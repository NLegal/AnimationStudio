# Phase 5 — Audio Bible & Music Production System

## AI Nursery Studio

### Version 1.0

---

# Objective

Phase 5 defines the **entire audio identity** of the studio.

Characters are designed.

The world exists.

Assets are complete.

Animation rules are established.

Now we define **what the audience hears**.

This includes:

* Songs
* Singing
* Character voices
* Narration
* Dialogue
* Sound effects
* Foley
* Background ambience
* Mixing
* Mastering
* Localization

The goal is to ensure that every episode sounds like it belongs to the same studio.

---

# Philosophy

Children recognize audio before visuals.

A recognizable melody, voice, or sound effect becomes part of your brand.

Your audio should be:

* Warm
* Cheerful
* Educational
* Memorable
* Positive
* Calm
* Clean
* High energy without being overwhelming

# Mixing Standards

Dialogue always takes priority.

Vocals remain intelligible over music.

Sound effects should support, not distract.

Maintain consistent loudness across episodes.

Avoid sudden volume spikes.

---

# Lip-Sync Standards

Songs and dialogue should use the same timing reference throughout the pipeline.

Standardize phoneme timing to simplify animation.

Maintain consistent mouth shapes for recurring sounds.

---

# Localization

Design the pipeline for future expansion.

Support:

* Multiple languages
* Alternate voice casts
* Localized songs
* Localized subtitles
* Region-specific pronunciations

Keep music stems where possible so vocals can be replaced without recreating the entire song.
